# Ranger's Bramble

*uncommon, plant, ranger, transmutation*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Area**: all squares that contain plants in a 5-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: APG p.235

---

You cause plants in the area to entangle your foes, with the effects of entangle. A creature that critically fails the save takes 2d4 persistent bleed damage in addition to being immobilized.

Escaping your bramble doesn't end the bleed damage.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The bleed damage on a critical failure increases by 1d4.
