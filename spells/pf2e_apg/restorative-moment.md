# Restorative Moment

*uncommon, hex, necromancy, witch*

- **Level**: Focus 5
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Source**: APG p.239

---

You manipulate the threads of time around the target, giving them some of the benefits of a day's passage. The target decreases the values of any doomed and drained conditions it has by 1 and can immediately attempt one saving throw against each affliction it has with a stage length of 1 day or less. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 day.
