# Bramble Bush

*uncommon, concentrate, manipulate, plant, wood*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: 5-foot cube
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Source**: WoW3 p.78

---

In a sudden burst of growth, you cause a thorned bush to sprout from the ground, lash around, and wither. Any creature in the area takes 1d4 piercing damage with a basic Reflex saving throw.

Until the start of your next turn, the area is difficult terrain and hazardous terrain. Any creature entering the square takes 1d4 piercing damage with a basic Reflex saving throw.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The initial damage increases by 1d4, and the damage dealt by hazardous terrain increases by 1.
