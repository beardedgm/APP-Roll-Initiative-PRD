# Rainbow's End

*rare, concentrate, light, manipulate, mythic, spirit*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, primal, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: 10-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: basic Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: WoI p.156

---

You reach upward to wrest down a rainbow and harness its power to connect this world to the heavens. Each creature in the area takes 1d4 spirit damage with a basic Fortitude save. Any creature that fails this save is additionally dazzled|PC1 for 1 round. For the spell's duration, an ally who's adjacent to you can Interact|PC1 and be instantly teleported to an unoccupied space in the spell's area, as long as they don't travel more than 60 feet. This effect has the teleportation trait.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 2d4, the duration of the dazzled|PC1 condition on a failed save increases by 1 round, and the maximum distance an ally can use the rainbow to teleport increases by 10 feet.
