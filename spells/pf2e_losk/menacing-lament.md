# Menacing Lament

*uncommon, archetype, auditory, concentrate, emotion, focus, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30-foot emanation
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Source**: LOSK p.147

---

You wail a dirge for your enemies. Enemies in the area are off-guard|PC1 against your attacks and gain no benefits from flanking you for the duration.
