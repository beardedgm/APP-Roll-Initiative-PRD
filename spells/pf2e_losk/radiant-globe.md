# Radiant Globe

*uncommon, concentrate, light, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 10-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: LOSK p.53

---

You create a dome of brilliant light that destroys projectiles attempting to pass through it. Ammunition from physical ranged attacks—such as arrows, bolts, sling bullets, and other objects of similar size—is destroyed in a flash of light when it passes into or out of the globe's area. Attackers targeting creatures or objects through the globe's surface with physical ranged attacks must succeed at a Fortitude save or become dazzled|PC1 for 1 round (blinded|PC1 for 1 round on a critical failure).
