# Solid Fog

*conjuration, water*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆ (material, somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute (dismissible)
- **Source**: CRB p.370

---

You conjure a bank of fog so thick it impedes movement as well as sight. This functions as obscuring mist, except that the area is also terrain. You can Dismiss the spell.
