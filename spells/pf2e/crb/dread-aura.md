# Dread Aura

*uncommon, enchantment, aura, emotion, fear, mental, wizard*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Area**: 30-foot-radius emanation centered on you
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: CRB p.406

---

You emit an aura of terror. Foes in the area are frightened 1 and unable to reduce the condition.
