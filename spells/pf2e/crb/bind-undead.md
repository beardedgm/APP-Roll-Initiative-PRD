# Bind Undead

*necromancy*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 mindless undead creature with a level no greater than bind undead's spell level
- **Duration**: 1 day
- **Source**: CRB p.321

---

With a word of necromantic power, you seize control of the target. It gains the minion trait. If you or an ally uses any hostile actions against the target, the spell ends.
