# Allegro

*uncommon, bard, cantrip, composition, emotion, enchantment, mental*

- **Level**: Focus 7
- **Cast**: ◆ (verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 ally
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Source**: CRB p.386

---

You perform rapidly, speeding up your ally. The ally becomes quickened and can use the additional action to Strike, Stride, or Step.
