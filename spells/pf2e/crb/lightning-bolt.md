# Lightning Bolt

*electricity, evocation*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Area**: 120-foot line
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Source**: CRB p.348

---

A bolt of lightning strikes outward from your hand, dealing 4d12 electricity damage.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d12.
