# Dragon Breath

*uncommon, evocation, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Area**: 30-foot cone or 60-foot line originating from you or 10-foot burst within 30 feet of you
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex or Fortitude
- **Source**: CRB p.403

---

You spew energy from your mouth, dealing 5d6 damage. The area, damage type, and save depend on the dragon type in your bloodline.

Dragon Type | Area and Damage Type | Saving Throw
search=black dragon, search=brine dragon1 or search=copper dragon | 60-foot line of acid | Reflex
search=blue dragon or search=bronze dragon | 60-foot line of electricity | Reflex
search=brass dragon | 60-foot line of fire | Reflex
search=cloud dragon | 30-foot cone of electricity | Reflex
search=crystal dragon1 or search=forest dragon1 | 30-foot cone of piercing | Reflex
search=green dragon | 30-foot cone of poison | Fortitude
search=gold dragon, search=magma dragon1 or search=red dragon | 30-foot cone of fire | Reflex
search=sea dragon1 | 10-foot burst within 30 feet of bludgeoning | Reflex
search=silver dragon or search=white dragon | 30-foot cone of cold | Reflex
search=sky dragon1 | 10-foot burst within 30 feet of electricity | Reflex
search=sovereign dragon1 | 30-foot cone of mental | Will
search=umbral dragon1 | 30-foot cone of negative | Reflex
search=underworld dragon1 | 10-foot burst within 30 feet of fire | Reflex

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
