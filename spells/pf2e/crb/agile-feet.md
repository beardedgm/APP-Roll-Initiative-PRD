# Agile Feet

*uncommon, cleric, transmutation*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆ (somatic)
- **Duration**: until the end of the current turn
- **Source**: CRB p.389

---

The blessings of your god make your feet faster and your movements more fluid. You gain a +5-foot status bonus to your Speed and ignore terrain. As part of casting agile feet, you can Stride or Step; you can instead Burrow, Climb, Fly, or Swim if you have the appropriate Speed.
