# Lose the Path

*illusion, mental, visual*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction (somatic)
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: the triggering creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: SoM p.113

---

You surround a moving creature with lifelike illusions, shifting their perception of the terrain to subtly lead them off course. The target must attempt a Will save.

Regardless of the result, the creature is immune to lose the path for 1 hour.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature treats all squares as terrain for its Stride.

**Critical Failure** As failure, except that you determine where the target moves during the Stride, though you can't move it into hazardous terrain or to a place it can't stand.
