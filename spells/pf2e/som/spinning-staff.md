# Spinning Staff

*uncommon, evocation, magus*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆ (verbal)
- **Source**: SoM p.144

---

You spin your staff with a twirling flourish, attacking two foes and transforming the momentum into magical energy to charge for your next Spellstrike|SoM. Make a melee Strike with your staff against one foe and then a second melee Strike with your staff against a different foe. Your multiple attack penalty applies to both of these attacks as normal.
