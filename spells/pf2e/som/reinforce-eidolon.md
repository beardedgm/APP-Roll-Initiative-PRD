# Reinforce Eidolon

*uncommon, abjuration, cantrip, summoner*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆ (verbal)
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Targets**: your eidolon
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Source**: SoM p.145

---

You focus deeply on the link between you and your eidolon and reinforce your eidolon's defenses. Your eidolon gains a +1 status bonus to AC and saving throws, plus resistance to all damage equal to half the spell's level. Your eidolon can benefit from either boost eidolon|SoM or reinforce eidolon|SoM, but not both; if you cast one of these spells during the other's duration, the newer spell replaces the older one.
