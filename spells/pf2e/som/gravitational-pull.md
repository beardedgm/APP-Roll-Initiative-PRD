# Gravitational Pull

*evocation*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: 1 to 3 (somatic, somatic, verbal, somatic, material, verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Source**: SoM p.109

---

By suddenly altering gravity, you pull the target toward you.

The target is pulled 10 feet closer to you unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save. On a critical failure, it's also knocked prone. The effects of this spell change depending on the number of actions you spend when you Cast this Spell.

1 (somatic) The spell targets one creature.

2 (somatic, verbal) The spell targets one creature and pulls the target 20 feet instead of 10.

3 (material, somatic, verbal) The spell targets up to 5 creatures.
