# Mind of Menace

*contingency, emotion, enchantment, fear, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: 10 minutes (material, somatic, verbal)
- **Duration**: 24 hours
- **Source**: SoM p.116

---

This spell wards against those who attempt to subvert your mind and turns mental magic back on them. When the spell is complete, you gain the Fight with Fear reaction; once you use the reaction, the spell ends.

The triggering creature must attempt a Will save, which has the following effects.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2, and you gain a +2 status bonus to your saving throw or other defense against the triggering mental effect.

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 2, and you're unaffected by the triggering mental effect.
