# Mud Pit

*conjuration, earth, water*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆ (material, somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: squares on the ground in a 15-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: SoM p.117

---

Thick, clinging mud covers the ground, 1 foot deep. The mud is terrain.
