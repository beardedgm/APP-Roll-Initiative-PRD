# Stone Lance

*uncommon, attack, earth, evocation*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: SoM p.201

---

You conjure a jagged lance of stone and then launch it at a foe. Make a spell attack roll against the target.

On a hit, you deal 6d6 piercing damage and the lance impales the creature, giving it a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds unless it Escapes. On a critical hit, if the creature is on the ground, the lance also embeds into the ground and immobilizes the creature until it Escapes. A creature that Escapes after being impaled takes 3 persistent bleed damage. When the spell ends, the lance crumbles into dirt, freeing the target if it hasn't Escaped.

---

**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 2d6 and the persistent bleed damage for Escapes by 1.
