# Phantom Prison

*illusion, incapacitation, mental, visual*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆ (material, somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 50 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: SoM p.121

---

You completely surround a Large or smaller creature in immobile illusory walls, trapping it inside a false prison it can't escape. No other creatures see or feel these walls, and the target can't see anything outside of the illusory walls. The target can attempt a Will save when the spell is cast, each time it attempts to interact with or escape the walls, and each time something from outside the walls affects the target. On a success, it disbelieves the illusion|CRB|2|disbelieving illusions|0 and the spell ends.

---

**Heightened (8th)** You can target up to 5 creatures.
