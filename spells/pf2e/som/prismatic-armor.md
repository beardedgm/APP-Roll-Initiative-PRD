# Prismatic Armor

*abjuration*

- **Level**: Spell 7
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 willing creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: SoM p.122

---

You wrap the target in armor made of multicolored light.

It functions as chromatic armor|som that is all colors (granting resistance 5 to acid, electricity, fire, force, mental, poison, and sonic). An attacker that critically fails on its saving throw against the spell is blinded rather than dazzled.

---

**Heightened (9th)** The resistances increase to 10 each.
