# Soothing Mist

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, healing, manipulate, ranger, vitality*

- **Level**: Focus 2
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 willing living creature or 1 undead creature
- **Source**: PC1 p.383

---

You call forth a magical mist that envelops a creature. The mist restores 2d8 Hit Points to a target living creature and ends one source of persistent damage|PC1|persistent acid, bleed, fire, poison, or void damage affecting it. If the creature is taking persistent damage|PC1 from multiple sources, you select which one is removed. Against an undead target, you deal 2d8 vitality damage (basic Fortitude save); if it fails the save, it also takes 2 persistent damage|PC1|persistent vitality damage.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing (or damage to an undead target) increases by 1d8, and the persistent damage|PC1|persistent vitality damage to an undead creature increases by 1.
