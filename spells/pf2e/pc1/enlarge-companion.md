# Enlarge Companion

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, manipulate, polymorph, ranger*

- **Level**: Focus 2
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: your animal companion
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.383

---

Your animal companion grows much larger, towering over its foes in battle. Your animal companion becomes Large, gaining the effects of a 2nd-rank enlarge|PC1 spell.

---

**Heightened (4th)** Your animal companion instead becomes Huge, gaining the benefits of a 4th-rank enlarge|PC1 spell.
