# Hand of the Apprentice

*uncommon, attack, focus, manipulate, wizard*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 500 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: AC
- **Source**: PC1 p.388

---

You take advantage of one of the most fundamental lessons of magic to levitate and propel your weapon. You hurl a held melee weapon with which you are trained at the target, making a spell attack roll. On a success, you deal the weapon's damage as if you had hit with a melee Strike|PC1, but add your spellcasting attribute modifier to damage, rather than your Strength modifier. On a critical success, you deal double damage, and you add the weapon's critical specialization effect. Regardless of the outcome, the weapon flies back to you and returns to your hand.
