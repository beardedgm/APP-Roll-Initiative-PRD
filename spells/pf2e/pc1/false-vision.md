# False Vision

*uncommon, concentrate, illusion, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: 10 minutes
- **Range**: touch
- **Area**: 100-foot burst
- **Duration**: until your next daily preparations
- **Source**: PC1 p.330

---

You create a false image that fools any attempts to scry on an area. Any scrying|PC1 spell sees, hears, smells, and otherwise detects whatever you wish within the area, rather than what is actually in the area. You can Sustain|PC1 the Spell each round to change the illusion as you desire, including playing out a complex scene. If the scrying|PC1 spell is of a higher level than false vision, the scryer can attempt a Perception|PC1 check to disbelieve the illusion|CRB|2|disbelieving illusions|0, though even if they're successful, they can't learn what's truly going on in the area.
