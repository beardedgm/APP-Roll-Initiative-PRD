# Gravity Weapon

*uncommon, focus, ranger*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.383

---

You build up gravitational force and funnel it into your blows, leading to more powerful attacks with blade and bow alike. On your first weapon Strike|PC1 each round, you gain a status bonus to damage equal to twice the number of weapon damage dice.
