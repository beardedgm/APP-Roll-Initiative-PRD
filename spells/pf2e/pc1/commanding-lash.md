# Commanding Lash

*uncommon, cleric, concentrate, focus, linguistic, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Targets**: a creature you dealt damage to on your most recent action
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: PC1 p.380

---

With the threat of more pain, you compel a creature you've recently harmed. You issue a command to the target, with the effects of the spell command. If the target is frightened|PC1, stupefied|PC1, or taking persistent damage|PC1, it takes a –2 circumstance penalty to the save. Regardless of the result, the target is then temporarily immune for 1 hour.
