# Caustic Blast

*acid, cantrip, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: 5-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Source**: PC1 p.319

---

You fling a large glob of acid that immediately detonates, spraying nearby creatures. Creatures in the area take 1d8 acid damage with a basic Reflex save; on a critical failure, the creature also takes 1 persistent damage|PC1|persistent acid damage.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The initial damage increases by 1d8|1,3,5,7,9|1d8, and the persistent damage|PC1|persistent damage on a critical failure increases by 1.
