# Daze

*cantrip, concentrate, manipulate, mental, nonlethal*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Source**: PC1 p.322

---

You push into the target's mind and daze it with a mental jolt. The jolt deals 1d6 mental damage, with a basic Will save. If the target critically fails the save, it is also stunned 1|PC1.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6|1-10|1d6.
