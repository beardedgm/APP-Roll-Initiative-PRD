# Synaptic Pulse

*concentrate, incapacitation, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 30-foot emanation
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Source**: PC1 p.362

---

You emit a pulsating mental blast that penetrates the minds of all enemies in the area. Each enemy in the area must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is stunned 1|PC1.

**Failure** The creature is stunned 2|PC1.

**Critical Failure** The creature is stunned|PC1 for 1 round.
