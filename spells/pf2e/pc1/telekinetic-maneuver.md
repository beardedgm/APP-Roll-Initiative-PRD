# Telekinetic Maneuver

*attack, concentrate, force, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Source**: PC1 p.363

---

With a rush of telekinetic power, you move a foe or something they carry. You can attempt to Disarm|PC1, Reposition|PC1, Shove|PC1, or Trip|PC1 the target using a spell attack roll instead of an Athletics|PC1 check.
