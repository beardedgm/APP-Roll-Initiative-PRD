# Spiral of Horrors

*uncommon, aura, concentrate, emotion, fear, focus, manipulate, mental, wizard*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 30-foot emanation
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.388

---

Shades and spirits howl and whirl around you in a display that strikes fear into the hearts of all who witness it. Enemies in the area are frightened|PC1|frightened 1 and can't reduce their frightened|PC1 value below 1 for the spell's duration.
