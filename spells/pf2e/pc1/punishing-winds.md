# Punishing Winds

*air, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 8
- **Traditions**: primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Area**: 30-foot radius, 100-foot-tall cylinder
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.351

---

Violent winds and a powerful downdraft fill the area, forming a cyclone. All flying creatures in the area descend 40 feet. The entire area is terrain for Flying creatures, and terrain for creatures on the ground or Climbing. Any creature that ends its turn Flying within the area descends 20 feet. Any creature pushed into a surface by this spell's winds takes bludgeoning damage as though it had fallen.

The squares at the outside vertical edges of the cylinder prevent creatures from leaving. These squares are terrain, and a creature attempting to push through must succeed at an Athletics|PC1 check or Acrobatics|PC1 check to Maneuver in Flight|PC1 against your spell DC to get through. A creature that fails ends its current action but can try again.
