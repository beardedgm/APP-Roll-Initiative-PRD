# Summon Celestial

*concentrate, holy, manipulate, summon*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: divine
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.360

---

You summon a creature that has the celestial trait and whose level is 5 to fight for you. The GM might determine your deity restricts the specific types of celestials you can summon in certain cases. For instance, Calistria doesn't typically allow her followers to summon aeons.

---

**Heightened (+1)** As listed on the summon trait.
