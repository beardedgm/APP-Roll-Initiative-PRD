# Deafness

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Source**: PC1 p.322

---

The target loses hearing; it must attempt a Fortitude save. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is deafened|PC1 for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is deafened|PC1 for 10 minutes.

**Critical Failure** The target is deafened|PC1 permanently.
