# Mariner's Curse

*concentrate, curse, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: PC1 p.342

---

You afflict the target with the curse of the roiling, unforgiving sea. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target becomes sickened 1|PC1. Reducing its sickened|PC1 condition to 0 ends the curse.

**Failure** The target becomes sickened 1|PC1 and can't reduce its sickened|PC1 condition below 1 while the curse remains. The curse can be lifted by remove curse or similar magic. Whenever the target is sickened|PC1 and on the water at least a mile from shore, it is also slowed 1|PC1.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target becomes sickened 2|PC1.
