# Clear Mind

*concentrate, healing, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 willing creature
- **Source**: PC1 p.320

---

You drive mental contamination from the target's mind. Attempt to counteracting an effect of your choice imposing one of these conditions on the target: fleeing|PC1, frightened|PC1, and stupefied|PC1. If you failed to counteract the effect but you would have if its counteract rank were 2 lower, instead suppress the effect until the beginning of your next turn. The effect's duration doesn't elapse while it's suppressed. This spell can't counteract or suppress conditions that are part of curses, diseases, or a natural state of the target.

---

**Heightened (4th)** Add confused|PC1, controlled|PC1, and slowed|PC1 to the list of conditions.

**Heightened (6th)** Add doomed|PC1 to the list of conditions.

**Heightened (8th)** Add stunned|PC1 to the list of conditions.
