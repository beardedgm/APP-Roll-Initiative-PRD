# Curse of Death

*uncommon, concentrate, curse, death, focus, hex, manipulate, void, witch*

- **Level**: Focus 5
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude or Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.384

---

Your patron wraps a hand around your target's heart. The target must attempt a Fortitude saving throw. Regardless of the result, the target is temporarily immune to all curses of death for 1 day.
