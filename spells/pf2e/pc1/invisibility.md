# Invisibility

*illusion, manipulate, subtle*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: PC1 p.339

---

Illusions bend light around the target, rendering it invisible|PC1. This makes it undetected|PC1 to all creatures, though the creatures can attempt to find the target, making it hidden|PC1 to them instead. If the target uses a hostile|PC1 action, the spell ends after that hostile action is completed.
