# Breath of Life

*concentrate, healing, vitality*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: divine
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: the triggering creature
- **Source**: PC1 p.319

---

Your blessing revives a creature at the moment of its death. You prevent the target from dying|PC1 and restore 5d8 Hit Points to the target. You can't use breath of life if the triggering effect was disintegrate|PC1 or a death effect.
