# Blindness

*concentrate, incapacitation, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Source**: PC1 p.318

---

You blind the target. The effect is determined by the target's Fortitude save. The target then becomes temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is blinded|PC1 until its next turn begins.

**Failure** The target is blinded|PC1 for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is blinded|PC1 permanently.
