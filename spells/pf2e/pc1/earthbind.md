# Earthbind

*concentrate, earth, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: 1 flying creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC1 p.327

---

Using the weight of earth, you hamper a target's flight, with effects based on its Fortitude save. If the creature reaches the ground safely, it doesn't take falling damage.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target falls safely up to 120 feet.

**Failure** The target falls safely up to 120 feet. If it hits the ground, it can't Fly|PC1, levitate, or otherwise leave the ground for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The target falls safely up to 120 feet. If it hits the ground, it can't Fly|PC1, levitate, or otherwise leave the ground for 1 minute.
