# Personal Blizzard

*uncommon, cold, focus, hex, witch*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude or Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.385

---

Your patron's breath becomes a blizzard of obscuring, scouring ice that follows your target. The target attempts a Fortitude save.
