# Flame Barrier

*uncommon, cleric, concentrate, focus*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: one creature that would take fire damage from the triggering effect
- **Source**: PC1 p.375

---

You swiftly deflect incoming flames. The target gains fire resistance 15 against the triggering effect.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 5.
