# Ode to Ouroboros

*uncommon, bard, composition, concentrate, focus*

- **Level**: Focus 5
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: the triggering creature
- **Source**: PC1 p.371

---

Your ode staves off death. The target's dying|PC1 condition remains 1 below the value at which it dies. This doesn't help prevent death from effects that kill the target without increasing its dying|PC1 condition, such as disintegrate and death effects.
