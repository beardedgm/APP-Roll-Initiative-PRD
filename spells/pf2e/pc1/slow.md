# Slow

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC1 p.357

---

You dilate the flow of time around the target, slowing its actions.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is slowed 1|PC1 for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is slowed 1|PC1 for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is slowed 2|PC1 for 1 minute.

---

**Heightened (6th)** You can target up to 10 creatures.
