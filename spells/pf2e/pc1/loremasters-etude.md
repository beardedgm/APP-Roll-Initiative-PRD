# Loremaster's Etude

*uncommon, bard, focus, fortune, manipulate*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◇ Free Action
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: you or the triggering ally
- **Source**: PC1 p.371

---

You call upon your muse's deep mysteries, granting the target a greater ability to think and recall information. Roll the triggering Recall Knowledge|PC1 skill check twice and use the higher result.
