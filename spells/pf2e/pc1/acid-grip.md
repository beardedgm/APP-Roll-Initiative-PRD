# Acid Grip

*acid, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Reflex
- **Source**: PC1 p.316

---

An ephemeral, taloned hand grips the target, burning it with magical acid. The target takes 2d8 acid damage plus 1d6 persistent damage|PC1|persistent acid damage depending on its Reflex save. A creature taking persistent damage|PC1 from this spell takes a –10-foot status bonus to its Speeds.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage and no persistent damage|PC1, and the claw moves it up to 5 feet in a direction of your choice.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and persistent damage|PC1, and the claw moves it up to 10 feet in a direction of your choice.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and full persistent damage|PC1, and the claw moves it up to 20 feet in a direction of your choice.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The initial damage increases by 2d8|4,6,8,10|2d8, and the persistent damage|PC1|persistent acid damage increases by 1d6|4,6,8,10|1d6.
