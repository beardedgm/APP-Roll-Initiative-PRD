# One with Stone

*concentrate, earth, manipulate, polymorph*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Duration**: 10 minutes or 8 hours
- **Source**: PC1 p.347

---

You can either transform into a stone or merge with stone. This spell has the same effects as one with plants|PC1, but lets you merge with or turn into stone. A stone you turn into has AC 23. You can Dismiss|PC1 this spell.
