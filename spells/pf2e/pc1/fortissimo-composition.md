# Fortissimo Composition

*uncommon, bard, concentrate, focus, spellshape*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◇ Free Action
- **Source**: PC1 p.370

---

You call upon your muse to greatly increase the benefits you provide with your courageous anthem|PC1, rallying anthem|PC1, or song of strength|PC1 composition. If your next action is to cast one of these compositions, attempt a Performance|PC1 check. The DC is usually the highest Will DC of the composition's targets, but the GM can assign a different DC based on the circumstances.

The effect of your composition depends on the result of your check.
