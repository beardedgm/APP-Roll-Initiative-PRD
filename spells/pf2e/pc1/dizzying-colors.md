# Dizzying Colors

*concentrate, illusion, incapacitation, manipulate, visual*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 15-foot cone
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 or more rounds (see below)
- **Source**: PC1 p.326

---

You unleash a swirling multitude of colors that overwhelms creatures based on their Will saves.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is dazzled|PC1 for 1 round.

**Failure** The creature is stunned 1|PC1, blinded|PC1 for 1 round, and dazzled|PC1 for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The creature is stunned|PC1 for 1 round and blinded|PC1 for 1 minute.
