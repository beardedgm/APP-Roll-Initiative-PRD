# Sigil

*cantrip, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature or object
- **Duration**: unlimited (see below)
- **Source**: PC1 p.357

---

You harmlessly place your unique magical sigil, which is about 1 square inch in size, on the targeted creature or object. The mark can be visible or invisible|PC1, and you can change it from one state to another by using an Interact|PC1 action to touch the target. The mark can be scrubbed or scraped off with 5 minutes of work. If it's on a creature, it fades naturally over the course of a week. The time before the mark fades increases depending on your heightened level.

---

**Heightened (3rd)** The sigil instead fades after 1 month.

**Heightened (5th)** The sigil instead fades after 1 year.

**Heightened (7th)** The sigil never fades.
