# Hymn of Healing

*uncommon, bard, composition, concentrate, focus, healing, vitality*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: you or 1 ally
- **Duration**: 4 rounds
- **Source**: PC1 p.370

---

Your glorious singing mends wounds and provides a temporary respite from harm. The target gains fast healing 2. When you Cast the Spell and the first time each round you Sustain|PC1|Sustain the Spell, the target gains 2 temporary Hit Points, which last for 1 round.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The fast healing and temporary Hit Points each increase by 2.
