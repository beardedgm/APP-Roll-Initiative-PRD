# Ranger's Bramble

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, manipulate, plant, ranger*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Area**: all squares that contain plants in a 5-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.384

---

You cause plants in the area to entangle your foes, with the effects of entangling flora. A creature that critically fails the save takes 2d4 persistent damage|PC1|persistent bleed damage in addition to being immobilized|PC1. Escaping your bramble doesn't end the bleed damage.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The bleed damage on a critical failure increases by 1d4.
