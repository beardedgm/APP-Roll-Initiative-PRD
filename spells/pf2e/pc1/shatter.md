# Shatter

*concentrate, manipulate, sonic*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 unattended object
- **Source**: PC1 p.356

---

A high-frequency sonic attack shatters a nearby object. You deal 2d10 sonic damage to the object, ignoring the object's Hardness if it is 4 or lower.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d10|2-10|1d10, and the Hardness the spell ignores increases by 2.
