# Touch of Undeath

*uncommon, cleric, focus, manipulate, void*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 living creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude or Fortitude
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC1 p.380

---

You attack the target's life force with undeath, dealing 1d6 void damage. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.
