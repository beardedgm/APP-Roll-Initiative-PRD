# Nature's Bounty

*uncommon, cleric, focus, manipulate, plant, vitality*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Source**: PC1 p.377

---

A palm-sized raw fruit or vegetable of your choice appears in your open hand. A creature can consume the food with an Interact|PC1 action to regain 3d10+12 Hit Points and be nourished as if it had eaten a meal. If uneaten, the food crumbles to dust after 1 minute.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The Hit Points restored increase by 6.
