# Disguise Magic

*concentrate, illusion, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: 1 minute
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 item or spell effect
- **Duration**: until your next daily preparations
- **Source**: PC1 p.324

---

You alter how an item's or spell's magical aura appears to effects like detect magic|PC1. You can hide the auras entirely, have an item register as a common item of lower level, or make a spell register as a common spell of the same or lower rank. You can Dismiss|PC1 the spell.

A caster using detect magic|PC1 or read aura|PC1 of an equal or higher rank can attempt to disbelieve the illusion|CRB|2|disbelieving illusions|0 from magic aura using the skill matching the tradition of the spell (Arcana|PC1 for arcane, Religion|PC1 for divine, Occultism|PC1 for occult, or Nature|PC1 for primal). Further attempts by the same caster get the same result as the initial check to disbelieve.

---

**Heightened (2nd)** You can Cast this Spell on a creature, disguising all items and spell effects on it.
