# Unfathomable Song

*auditory, concentrate, emotion, fear, incapacitation, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 9
- **Traditions**: occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: up to 5 creatures
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.364

---

Fleeting notes of a strange and unnatural song fill the air, overtaking the mind. Each target must attempt a Will save when you cast the spell, and again the first time you Sustain|PC1 this Spell each round. A creature needs to attempt only one save against the song each round, and you have to keep the same targets when you Sustain|PC1 the Spell.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected, can't be affected on subsequent rounds, and is temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Success** The target is unaffected this round, but it can be affected on subsequent rounds.

**Failure** Roll onTable on the table below.

**Critical Failure** Roll onTable on the table below.

Result | Effect
1 | The target is frightened|PC1|frightened 2.
2 | The target is confused|PC1 for 1 round.
3 | The target is stupefied|PC1|stupefied 4 for 1 round.
4 | The target is blinded|PC1 for 1 round.
5 | The target is stunned|PC1 for 1 round and stupefied|PC1|stupefied 1 for an unlimited duration.
