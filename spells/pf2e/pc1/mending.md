# Mending

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: 10 minutes
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: non-magical object of light Bulk or less
- **Source**: PC1 p.343

---

You repair the target item. You restore 5 Hit Points per spell level to the target, potentially removing the broken|PC1 condition if this repairs it past the item's Broken|PC1 Threshold. You can't replace lost pieces or repair an object that's been completely destroyed.

---

**Heightened (2nd)** You can target a non-magical object of 1 Bulk or less.

**Heightened (3rd)** You can target a non-magical object of 2 Bulk or less, or a magical object of 1 Bulk or less.
