# Unimpeded Stride

*uncommon, cleric, focus, manipulate*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Source**: PC1 p.375

---

Divine grace ensure that nothing can keep you prisoner or hold you back. You immediately escape from every magical effect that has you immobilized|PC1 or grabbed|PC1 unless the effect is of a higher rank than your unimpeded stride|PC1 spell. You then Stride|PC1. During this movement, you ignore difficult terrain and any circumstance or status penalties to your Speed.
