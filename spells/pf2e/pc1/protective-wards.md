# Protective Wards

*uncommon, aura, focus, manipulate, wizard*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Area**: 5-foot emanation centered on you
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.387

---

You expand a ring of glyphs that shields your allies. You and any allies in the area gain a +1 status bonus to AC. Each time you Sustain|PC1|Sustain the Spell, the emanation's radius increases by 5 feet, to a maximum of 30 feet.
