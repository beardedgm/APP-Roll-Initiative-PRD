# Goblin Pox

*disease, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Source**: PC1 p.333

---

Your touch afflicts the target with goblin pox, an irritating allergenic rash. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is sickened 1|PC1.

**Failure** The target is afflicted with goblin pox at stage 1.

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with goblin pox at stage 2.

**Goblin Pox**
**Stage 1** sickened 1|PC1 (1 round)
**Stage 2** sickened 1|PC1 and slowed 1|PC1 (1 round)
**Stage 3** sickened 1|PC1 and the creature can't reduce its sickened|PC1 value below 1 (1 day)
