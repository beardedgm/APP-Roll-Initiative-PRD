# Vitality Lash

*cantrip, concentrate, manipulate, vitality*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: divine, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature that is undead or otherwise has void healing
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Source**: PC1 p.366

---
