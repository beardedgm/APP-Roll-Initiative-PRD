# Magic's Vessel

*uncommon, cleric, focus, manipulate*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.376

---

A creature becomes a divine receptacle for pure magical energy. The target gains a +1 status bonus to saving throws. Each time you Cast a Spell from your spell slots, you automatically Sustain|PC1|Sustain this Spell and grant its target resistance to damage from spells equal to the spell's rank, until the start of your next turn.
