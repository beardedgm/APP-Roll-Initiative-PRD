# Divine Decree

*concentrate, manipulate, sanctified, spirit*

- **Level**: Spell 7
- **Traditions**: divine
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 40 feet
- **Area**: 40-foot emanation
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC1 p.325

---

You utter a potent litany from your faith, a mandate that harms those who oppose your ideals. You deal 7d10 spirit damage to your enemies in the area; each enemy must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is enfeebled 2|PC1 for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is enfeebled 2|PC1 for 1 minute. On your home plane and the creature is not, the creature is sent back to its home plane. A creature of 10th-level or lower must attempt a Will save or be paralyzed|PC1 for 1 minute; if it critical fails, it dies.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 7d10|7-10|1d10, and the level of creatures that must attempt a second save on a critical failure increases by 2.
