# Telekinetic Hand

*cantrip, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 unattended object of light Bulk or less
- **Source**: PC1 p.362

---

You create a floating, magical hand, either invisible|PC1 or ghostlike, that grasps the target object and levitates it slowly up to 20 feet in any direction. When you Sustain|PC1 the Spell, you can move the object an additional 20 feet. If the object is in the air when the spell ends, the object falls.

---

**Heightened (3rd)** You can target an unattended object with a Bulk of 1 or less.

**Heightened (5th)** The range increases to 60 feet, and you can target an unattended object with a Bulk of 1 or less.

**Heightened (7th)** The range increases to 60 feet, and you can target an unattended object with a Bulk of 2 or less.
