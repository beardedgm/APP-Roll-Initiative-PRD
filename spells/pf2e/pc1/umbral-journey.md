# Umbral Journey

*uncommon, concentrate, manipulate, shadow, teleportation*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: 1 minute
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: you and up to 10 willing creatures touched
- **Duration**: 8 hours (dismissible)
- **Source**: PC1 p.364

---

You move partially into the Netherworld, using its warped nature to speed your travels. Each hour, you cover roughly as much ground as you normally would in 3 days. The landmarks are vague and symbolic rather than concrete, leaving you within a mile of your intended destination when you Dismiss|PC1 the spell or its duration ends.
