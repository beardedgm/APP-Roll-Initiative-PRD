# Wave of Despair

*concentrate, emotion, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 30-foot cone
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 or more rounds
- **Source**: PC1 p.369

---

You inflict despair on creatures in the area. The effects for each creature are determined by its Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** For 1 round, the creature can't use reactions and must attempt another save at the start of its turn; on a failure, it is slowed 1|PC1 for that turn as it sobs uncontrollably.

**Failure** As success, but the slowed 1|PC1 duration is 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature is automatically slowed 1|PC1 for 1 minute.

---

**Heightened (7th)** The area increases to a 60-foot cone.
