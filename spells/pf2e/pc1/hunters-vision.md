# Hunter's Vision

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, ranger*

- **Level**: Focus 5
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 hunted prey
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: PC1 p.384

---

Your target glows with a magical aura visible only to you and those who follow your lead. Your target is visible to you and others sharing your Hunt Prey|PC1 benefits even if it wouldn't normally be due to lighting or the concealed|PC1 or invisible|PC1 conditions, though cover from opaque objects still blocks your sight. You ignore the flat check against the target due to the concealed|PC1 condition, and the target isn't automatically hidden|PC1 from you due to darkness or being invisible|PC1.
