# Veil of Confidence

*uncommon, cleric, concentrate, focus, mental*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.373

---

You surround yourself in confidence. You reduce your current frightened|PC1 condition by 1, and whenever you would become frightened|PC1 during the duration, reduce the amount by 1. If you critically fail a save against a fear effect, veil of confidence reduces your frightened|PC1 value from that effect, and then ends.
