# Stupefy

*concentrate, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC1 p.359

---

You dull the target's mind, depending on its Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is stupefied 1 until the start of your next turn.

**Failure** The target is stupefied 2|PC1 for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is stupefied 3|PC1 for 1 minute.
