# Evil Eye

*uncommon, cantrip, curse, hex, manipulate, witch*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.386

---

Your patron's resentment manifests in a baleful, envious gaze. The target becomes sickened|PC1|sickened 1 if it fails a Will save (or sickened|PC1|sickened 2 on a critical failure). This condition value can't be reduced below 1 while the spell is active and you can see the target.
