# Phantasmal Calamity

*concentrate, illusion, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 6
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 500 feet
- **Area**: 30-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: PC1 p.349

---

A vision of apocalyptic destruction fills the mind of each creature in the area. The vision deals 11d6 mental damage (basic Will save). On a critical failure, the creature must also succeed at a Reflex save or believe it's trapped (stuck in a fissure, adrift at sea, or some other fate in keeping with its vision). If it fails the second save, it's also stunned|PC1 for 1 minute. It can attempt a new Will save at the end of each of its turns, and on a success, it disbelieves the illusion|CRB|2|disbelieving illusions|0 and recovers from the stunned|PC1 condition.

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 11d6|6-10|2d6.
