# Zeal for Battle

*uncommon, cleric, concentrate, emotion, focus, fortune, mental*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction
- **Range**: 10 feet
- **Targets**: you and the triggering ally
- **Source**: PC1 p.381

---

You stoke the righteous anger within yourself and an ally. You and the target ally each roll a d20 and use the higher result for both your initiative rolls. You each still use your own Perception|PC1 modifier or other statistic to determine your results.
