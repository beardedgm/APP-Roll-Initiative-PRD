# Toxic Cloud

*concentrate, death, manipulate, poison*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: basic Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute (dismissible)
- **Source**: PC1 p.363

---

You conjure a poisonous fog. This functions as mist|PC1, except the area moves 10 feet away from you each round. A breathing creature that starts its turn in the area takes 6d8 poison damage with a basic Fortitude save. You can Dismiss|PC1 the spell.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 6d8|5-10|1d8.
