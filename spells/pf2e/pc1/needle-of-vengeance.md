# Needle of Vengeance

*uncommon, focus, hex, manipulate, mental, witch*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 enemy
- **Saving Throw**: basic Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.385

---

A long, jagged needle jabs into the target foe's psyche whenever it tries to attack a creature your patron holds in special regard.

Name yourself or one of your allies. The target takes 2 mental damage any time it uses a hostile|PC1 action against the named creature, with a basic Will save.

---

**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 2.
