# Warp Mind

*concentrate, emotion, incapacitation, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 7
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: PC1 p.368

---

You scramble a creature's mental faculties and sensory input.

The target must attempt a Will saving throw. Regardless of the result of that save, the target is then temporarily immune for 10 minutes. Warp mind's effects happen instantly, so dispel magic|PC1 and other effects that counteract spells can't counteract them. However, wish rituals and abilities that can remove non-magical effects can still counteract the effects.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target spends the first action on its next turn with the confused|PC1 condition.

**Failure** The target is confused|PC1 for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is confused|PC1 permanently.
