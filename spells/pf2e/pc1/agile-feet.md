# Agile Feet

*uncommon, cleric, focus, manipulate*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Source**: PC1 p.379

---

The blessings of your god make your feet faster and your movements more fluid. You gain a +5-foot status bonus to your Speed and ignore difficult terrain. As part of casting agile feet, you can Step|PC1, Stride|PC1, or Tumble Through|PC1; you can instead Burrow|PC1, Climb|PC1, Fly|PC1, or Swim|PC1 if you have the appropriate Speed.
