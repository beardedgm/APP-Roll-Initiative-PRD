# Charming Touch

*uncommon, cleric, emotion, focus, incapacitation, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature that could find you attractive
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: PC1 p.378

---

You infuse your target with attraction, causing it to act friendlier toward you. The target attempts a Will save. It gains a +4 circumstance bonus to this save if you or your allies recently threatened or were hostile|PC1 to it.
