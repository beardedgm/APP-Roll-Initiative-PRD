# Ring of Truth

*uncommon, concentrate, detection, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: PC1 p.354

---

You designate an area in which lies are revealed. Creatures in the area also take a –2 status penalty to Deception|PC1 checks. Each time a creature in the area speaks a true statement, the soft ring of a bell sounds in the area. Creatures are aware of the magic; therefore, they can avoid answering questions to which they would normally respond with a lie, or they can be evasive as long as they remain within the boundaries of the truth. If a creature is in the area when the spell is cast or later enters the area, that creature attempts a Will save. It uses the results of this initial save if it leaves and reenters the area.

**Critical Success** The target is so convincing that the bell rings even if they lie.

**Success** If the target lies and succeeds at their Deception|PC1 check against all targets, the bell still rings.

**Failure** The bell accurately sees through their deception|PC1 and will never ring if they lie.
