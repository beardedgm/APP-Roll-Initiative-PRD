# Telekinetic Haul

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: 1 unattended object of up to 80 Bulk with no dimension longer than 20 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.362

---

You move the target up to 20 feet, potentially suspending it in midair. When you Sustain|PC1 the Spell, you can do so again, or you can choose a different eligible target to move.
