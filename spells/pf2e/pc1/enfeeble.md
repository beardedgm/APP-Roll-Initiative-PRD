# Enfeeble

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC1 p.329

---

You sap the target's strength, depending on its Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target becomes enfeebled 1|PC1 until the start of your next turn.

**Failure** The target becomes enfeebled 2|PC1 for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target becomes enfeebled 3|PC1 for 1 minute.
