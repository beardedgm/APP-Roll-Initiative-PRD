# Song of Marching

*uncommon, bard, cantrip, composition, concentrate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Area**: 60-foot emanation
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Source**: PC1 p.371

---

You maintain a brisk performance that keeps allies on the move. You and your allies in the area can Hustle|PC1 for the spell's duration, in addition to your other exploration activities (your exploration activity is Sustain|PC1|Sustaining this Spell). You and your allies then become temporarily immune for 1 day.

If you enter an encounter while performing this song, you can use your Performance|PC1 modifier for the initiative roll. You and your affected allies also receive a +1 status bonus to that initiative roll.

---

**Heightened (6th)** You can Sustain|PC1|Sustain the Spell for up to 2 hours.

**Heightened (9th)** You can Sustain|PC1|Sustain the Spell for up to 4 hours
