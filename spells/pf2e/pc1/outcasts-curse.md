# Outcast's Curse

*concentrate, curse, manipulate, mental, misfortune*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: PC1 p.347

---

You afflict the target with a curse that makes its presence abrasive and off-putting. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** For 10 minutes, the target must roll twice and use the worse result whenever attempting a Deception|PC1, Diplomacy|PC1, Intimidation|PC1, or Performance|PC1 check, and creatures they encounter have an initial attitude toward them of one step worse (for instance, unfriendly|PC1 instead of indifferent|PC1).

**Failure** As success, but the effect is permanent.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and creatures that the target encounters have an initial attitude toward them of two steps worse.
