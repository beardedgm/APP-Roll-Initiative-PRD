# Forbidding Ward

*cantrip, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 ally and 1 enemy
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.332

---

You ward an ally against the attacks and hostile|PC1 spells from the target enemy. The target ally gains a +1 status bonus to Armor Class and saving throws against the target enemy's attacks, spells, and other effects.

---

**Heightened (6th)** The status bonus increases to +2.
