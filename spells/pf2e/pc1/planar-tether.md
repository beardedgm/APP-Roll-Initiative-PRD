# Planar Tether

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC1 p.350

---

You stitch the target to its current plane. While the target is affected by planar tether, the spell attempts to counteract|crb|3|counteracting any teleportation effect that would move the target, or any effect that would transport it to a different plane. Planar tether's duration is determined by the target's Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The duration is 1 minute.

**Failure** The duration is 10 minutes.

**Critical Failure** The duration is 1 hour.
