# Wails of the Damned

*auditory, concentrate, death, manipulate, void*

- **Level**: Spell 9
- **Traditions**: divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 40-foot emanation
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Source**: PC1 p.367

---

You howl the lament of damned souls. Each living enemy in the area takes 8d10 void damage depending on its Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes full damage.

**Failure** The target takes full damage and is drained|PC1 1d4.

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and is drained 4|PC1.
