# Magic Hide

*uncommon, abjuration, ranger*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆ (somatic)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: your animal companion
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: APG p.235

---

Your animal companion grows a thicker hide, matted fur, or a harder shell, granting it a +1 status bonus to AC.
