# Heart's Desire

*uncommon, emotion, illusion, mental, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: APG p.236

---

You surround your target with wondrous illusions of their greatest desires, which distract them from reality. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target disbelieves the illusion|CRB|2|disbelieving illusions|0 and is unaffected.

**Success** For 1 round, the target is stupefied 2 and takes a –2 status penalty to any checks it makes to determine the effects of reactions.

**Failure** For 1 minute, the target is stupefied 2 and can't use reactions.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and all spaces are terrain for the target.

---

**Heightened (+2)** You can target one additional creature.
