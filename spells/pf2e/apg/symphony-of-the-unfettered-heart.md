# Symphony of the Unfettered Heart

*uncommon, abjuration, bard, composition*

- **Level**: Focus 5
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: you or 1 ally
- **Source**: APG p.228

---

Your symphony lifts listeners from their worldly concerns.

Attempt a Performance check to counteract one of the following conditions affecting the target: grabbed, immobilized, paralyzed, restrained, slowed, or stunned. If you fail, you can't target that effect on the target for 1 day. Use the condition's source to determine the counteract DC (for example, the Escape DC for grabbed).

---

**Heightened (9th)** You can target up to four creatures.
