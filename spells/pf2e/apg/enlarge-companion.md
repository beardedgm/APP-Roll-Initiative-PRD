# Enlarge Companion

*uncommon, polymorph, ranger, transmutation*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: your animal companion
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: APG p.234

---

Your animal companion grows much larger, towering over its foes in battle. Your animal companion becomes Huge, gaining the effects of a 4th-level enlarge spell.
