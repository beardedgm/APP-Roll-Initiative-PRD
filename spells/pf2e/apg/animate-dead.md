# Animate Dead

*necromancy*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆ (material, somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: APG p.214

---

Your magic dredges up a corpse or skeleton and fills it with necromantic life, and you force the dead to fight at your command. You summon a common creature that has the undead trait and whose level is Creature Type=Undead; this creature gains the summoned trait. Heightening the spell increases the maximum level of creature you can summon.
