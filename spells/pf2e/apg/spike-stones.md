# Spike Stones

*conjuration, earth*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆ (material, somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Source**: APG p.225

---

Long, sharp spikes of solid rock thrust up from the ground in the area. The area becomes terrain and hazardous terrain.

A creature that moves on the ground through the area takes 3 piercing damage for every square of that area it moves into.

---

**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 1.
