# Life Boost

*uncommon, healing, hex, necromancy, witch*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆ (somatic)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 4 rounds
- **Source**: APG p.238

---

You temporarily bolster the target's vitality. The target gains fast healing equal to double the spell's level.
