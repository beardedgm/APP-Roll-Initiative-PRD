# Thunderburst

*uncommon, air, cursebound, evocation, oracle, sonic*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Source**: APG p.234

---

You create a powerful blast of air and a loud peal of thunder, dealing 2d6 bludgeoning damage and 2d6 sonic damage.

Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is deafened for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is deafened for 1 hour.

---

**Heightened (+2)** Increase each type of damage by 2d6 and the area by 5 feet.
