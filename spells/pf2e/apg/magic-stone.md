# Magic Stone

*necromancy, positive*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: divine, primal
- **Cast**: 1 to 3 (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 to 3 non-magical stones or sling bullets
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: APG p.222

---

You pour positive energy into ordinary stones, granting them temporary magical properties. You can target 1 non-magical stone or sling bullet for every action you use Casting this Spell.

The stones must be unattended or carried by you or a willing ally. The stones become +1 striking disrupting sling bullets. Each stone can be used only once, after which it crumbles to dust.
