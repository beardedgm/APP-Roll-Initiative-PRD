# Mind Games

*uncommon, enchantment, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: SoT1 p.77

---

You lock minds with the target, trying to mentally subdue them. The target must attempt a Will save. Each time you Sustain this Spell, the target attempts another Will save.

**Critical Success** You are stunned|CRB|stunned 1 and the spell ends.

**Success** The target is unaffected.

**Failure** The target is stunned|CRB|stunned 1.

**Critical Failure** The target is stunned|CRB|stunned 2.
