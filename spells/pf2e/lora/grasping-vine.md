# Grasping Vine

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, manipulate, plant, wizard, wood*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: 1 unattended object of up to 8 Bulk, or Medium or smaller creature that is currently on the ground
- **Saving Throw**: Reflex
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute (see text)
- **Source**: LORA p.32

---

A thick, curling vine erupts from the ground beneath you and stretches to the target, allowing you to move it around. If the target is an item, you can move it 10 feet in any direction within range when you Cast the Spell and the first time you Sustain|PC1 it each round. If the target is a creature, they must attempt a Reflex save.
