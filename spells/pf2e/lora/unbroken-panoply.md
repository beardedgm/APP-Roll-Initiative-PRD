# Unbroken Panoply

*uncommon, concentrate, manipulate, nonlethal*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 broken|PC1 weapon that is unattended or held by a willing creature
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Source**: LORA p.83

---

As tools of violence undone by violence, broken|PC1 weapons contain potent symbolic magic that faydhaans often call upon when forming alliances. Images of similar legendary weapons overlay the target, and the weapon's broken|PC1 condition is suppressed for the duration. The weapon gains the nonlethal trait during this time. The weapon's wielder can apply the weapon's item bonus to attack rolls, if any, to their Diplomacy|PC1 checks.

If the weapon would be damaged or broken|PC1 again, this spell ends.
