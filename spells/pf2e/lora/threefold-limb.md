# Threefold Limb

*uncommon, attack, concentrate, manipulate, morph, water*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: AC
- **Source**: LORA p.83

---

You temporarily transform one of your limbs into water, taking the form of ice, liquid water, or steam as you desire. Make a melee spell attack roll. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 damage; the type of damage dealt and any additional effect depends on the form you choose. On a critical hit, double the damage.

- **Ice** The limb deals cold damage, and the target takes a −10-foot status penalty to its Speeds until the start of your next turn. This spell gains the cold trait.
- **Liquid Water** The limb deals bludgeoning damage and you can Reposition|PC1 the target up to 10 feet.
- **Steam** The limb deals fire damage and steam clings to the target, making all creatures concealed|PC1 to them until the start of your next turn or they perform an Interact|PC1 action to wave the steam away. This spell gains the fire trait.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
