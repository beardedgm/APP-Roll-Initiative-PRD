# Halcyon Mists

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, manipulate, water*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 willing living creature
- **Source**: LORA p.68

---

In a moment of dire need, you call upon ancestral power to strengthen and sustain. A soothing mist gathers around the target, granting 1d8 temporary Hit Points and ending one source of persistent damage|PC1|persistent acid, bleed, fire, poison, or void damage affecting it. If the creature has persistent damage|PC1 from more than one of these sources, you choose the one that ends.

The mists linger around the target, making them concealed|PC1 until the beginning of their next turn, though this obvious concealment can't be used to Hide|PC1.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The temporary Hit Points increase by 1d8.
