# Unsettling Knowledge

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, linguistic, mental, wood*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: LORA p.32

---

You share a piece of hidden knowledge with a creature that is both upsetting and intriguing. When you Cast this Spell, choose Arcana|PC1, Nature|PC1, Occultism|PC1, Religion|PC1, or Society|PC1 as the focus of this knowledge. The target must attempt a Will save.
