# Fungal Infestation

*concentrate, fungus, manipulate, poison*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Area**: 15-foot cone
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Source**: PC2 p.245

---

Toxic spores swarm over creatures in the area, causing them to erupt in grotesque fungal growths. These noxious growths deal 2d6 persistent damage|PC1|persistent poison damage, and each creature must attempt a Fortitude save.
