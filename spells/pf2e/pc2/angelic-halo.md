# Angelic Halo

*uncommon, aura, concentrate, focus, holy, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Area**: 15-foot emanation
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.262

---

You gain an angelic halo with an aura that increases allies' healing from the heal|PC1 spell. Allies in your halo's emanation who are healed by a heal|PC1 spell gain a status bonus to Hit Points regained equal to double the heal|PC1 spell's level.
