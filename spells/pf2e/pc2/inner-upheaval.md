# Inner Upheaval

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, monk*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Source**: PC2 p.257

---

You focus your qi into devastating magical attacks. Make an unarmed Strike|PC1 or Flurry of Blows|PC1 (this doesn't change the limit on using only one flourish per turn). You gain a +1 status bonus to your attack rolls with the Strike|PC1|Strikes, and the Strike|PC1|Strikes deal 1d6 extra damage. This damage can be any of the following types of your choice, chosen each time you Strike|PC1: force, spirit, vitality, or void.

---

**Heightened (+4)** The extra damage increases by 1d6.
