# Arcane Countermeasure

*uncommon, focus, manipulate, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 5
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: the spell cast by the triggering creature
- **Source**: PC2 p.265

---

You undermine the target spell, making it easier to defend against. You reduce the spell's rank by 1, and targets of the spell gain a +2 status bonus to any saving throws, skill checks, AC, or DC against it.

You can't reduce the spell's rank below its minimum. For example, a 5th-rank howling blizzard|PC1 would remain 5th-rank, but a 5th-rank fireball|PC1 would become 4th-rank. Targets still gain all the other benefits, even if you don't reduce the spell's rank.
