# Fey Glamour

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, illusion, manipulate, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 5
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: 30-foot burst or Targets up to 10 willing creatures
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: PC2 p.264

---

You call upon fey glamours to cloak an area or the targets in illusion. This has the effect of either illusory scene|PC1 on the area or illusory disguise|PC1 on the creatures, as if heightened to a rank 1 rank lower than fey glamour, and using fey glamour's range and duration.
