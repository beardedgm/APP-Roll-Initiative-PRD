# Claim Undead

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, incapacitation, manipulate, oracle*

- **Level**: Focus 6
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 undead creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: PC2 p.259

---

You attempt to wrest control of a target undead or force it to recognize you as its master. If the target is controlled|PC1 by another creature, that controller attempts a Will saving throw to retain control; otherwise, the target must attempt a Will save.
