# Corrosive Muck

*acid, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: all squares in two 10-foot bursts
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.243

---

You create two puddles of acidic slime that hinders the movement of anyone who walks through it. The area of each pool becomes greater difficult terrain. Each round that a creature starts its turn in one of the pools or enters a pool during a move action it's using, it takes 8d6 acid damage with a basic Reflex save.

A creature that critically fails its saving throw also takes 1d6 persistent damage|PC1|persistent acid damage. As normal, if a Large or larger creature starts its turn in both pools, it takes the damage only once.

---

**Heightened (+2)** Create an additional 10-foot burst.
