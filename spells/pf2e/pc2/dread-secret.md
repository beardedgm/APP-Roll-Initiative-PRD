# Dread Secret

*uncommon, concentrate, emotion, fear, focus, manipulate, mental, oracle*

- **Level**: Focus 6
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: up to 6 creatures
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: PC2 p.261

---

You utter a powerful secret at odds with the fundamental nature of the target creatures. Choose a specific resistance or weakness with a numerical value that you believe one or more of the targets have due to Recall Knowledge|PC1 or previous experience with the targets, such as resistance to fire or weakness to silver.

If the information is incorrect for a given target, the spell has no effect on that target. Affected targets must attempt a Will saving throw.
