# Incendiary Aura

*uncommon, concentrate, fire, focus, manipulate, oracle*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 10-foot emanation
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.260

---

You surround yourself with a combustible aura. Each time a creature within the emanation takes fire damage, it catches on fire, taking 2d4 persistent damage|PC1|persistent fire damage.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The persistent damage|PC1 increases by 1d4 and the radius of the emanation increases by 5 feet.
