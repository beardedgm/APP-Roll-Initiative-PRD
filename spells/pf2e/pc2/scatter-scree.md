# Scatter Scree

*cantrip, concentrate, earth, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: two contiguous 5-foot cubes
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.250

---

A jumble of rocks cascades into the area. The scattering rocks deal 2d4 bludgeoning damage (basic Reflex save). The ground in the area becomes difficult terrain for the duration of the spell. A creature can Interact|PC1 to clear a square of this scree.

If you cast this spell again, any previous scatter scree you've cast ends.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
