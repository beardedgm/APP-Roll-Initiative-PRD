# Spectral Advance

*uncommon, champion, concentrate, focus, polymorph, spirit*

- **Level**: Focus 5
- **Cast**: 1 or 2
- **Source**: PC2 p.257

---

Taking on a spiritual form, you flash across the battlefield to engage an enemy. You Stride|PC1 to a space adjacent to an enemy. If you cast the spell using 2 actions, you can Stride|PC1 twice instead of once. If you have a fly Speed, you can Fly|PC1 instead of Striding. If you're mounted, you can have your mount move instead of you.

Movement from spectral advance doesn't trigger reactions and ignores difficult terrain and greater difficult terrain. During the movement, you (or your mount) have resistance equal to your level to all damage.
