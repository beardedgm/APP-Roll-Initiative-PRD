# Qi Rush

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, monk*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Source**: PC2 p.257

---

Accelerated by your qi, you move with such speed you become a blur. Move two times: two Stride|PC1|Strides, two Step|PC1|Steps, or one Stride|PC1 and one Step|PC1 (in either order). You gain the concealed|PC1 condition during this movement and until the start of your next turn.
