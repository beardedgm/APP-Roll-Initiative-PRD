# Wind Jump

*uncommon, air, concentrate, focus, monk*

- **Level**: Focus 5
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.258

---

You gain a fly Speed equal to your Speed. You must end your turn on solid ground, or you fall.

---

**Heightened (6th)** At the end of your turn, you can attempt a DC 30 Acrobatics|PC1 check to find purchase in midair. If you succeed, you don't fall.
