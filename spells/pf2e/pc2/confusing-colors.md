# Confusing Colors

*illusion, incapacitation, manipulate, subtle, visual*

- **Level**: Spell 8
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.243

---

A cloud of cascading, ever-changing colors manifests in the air.

Creatures are dazzled|PC1 while inside the cloud, as are those within 20 feet of the cloud's area. A creature must attempt a Will save if it is inside the cloud when you cast it, enters the cloud, ends its turn within the cloud, or uses a Seek|PC1 or Interact|PC1 action on the cloud. A creature currently affected by the cloud doesn't need to attempt new saves.
