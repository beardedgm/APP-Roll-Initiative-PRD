# Touch of Death

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, incapacitation, manipulate, monk*

- **Level**: Focus 8
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 month
- **Source**: PC2 p.257

---

Make a melee unarmed Strike|PC1. If you hit and the target is alive, anytime during the duration, you can spend a single action, which has the auditory and concentrate traits, to speak a word of death that could instantly slay it. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.
