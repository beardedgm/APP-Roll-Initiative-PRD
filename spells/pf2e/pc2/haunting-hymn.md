# Haunting Hymn

*auditory, cantrip, concentrate, manipulate, sonic*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 15-foot cone
- **Saving Throw**: basic Fortitude
- **Source**: PC2 p.246

---

You echo a jarring hymn that only creatures in the area can hear. The hymn deals 1d8 sonic damage, with a basic Fortitude save. If a target critically fails the save, it's also deafened|PC1 for 1 minute.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.
