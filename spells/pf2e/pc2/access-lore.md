# Access Lore

*uncommon, focus, fortune, manipulate, oracle*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.261

---

You sift through the multiverse's divine knowledge, seeking out tidbits related to a particular subject. Choose one Lore|PC1 skill. You temporarily gain the same proficiency rank in that Lore|PC1 skill as your proficiency rank for your oracle spellcasting.
