# Wild Winds Stance

*uncommon, air, focus, manipulate, monk, stance*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Duration**: until you leave the stance
- **Source**: PC2 p.258

---

You take on the stance of the flowing winds, sending out waves of energy at a distance. You can make wind crash unarmed Strike|PC1|Strikes as ranged Strike|PC1|Strikes against targets within 30 feet. These deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage; are in the brawling group; and have the agile, nonlethal, propulsive, and unarmed traits. Wind crash Strike|PC1|Strikes ignore concealment and all cover.

While in wild winds stance, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against ranged attacks.
