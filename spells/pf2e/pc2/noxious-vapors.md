# Noxious Vapors

*concentrate, manipulate, poison*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 10-foot emanation
- **Saving Throw**: basic Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Source**: PC2 p.248

---

You emit a cloud of toxic smoke that temporarily obscures you from sight. Each creature except you in the area when you Cast the Spell takes 1d6 poison damage (basic Fortitude save). A creature that critically fails the saving throw also becomes sickened|PC1|sickened 1. All creatures in the area become concealed|PC1, and all creatures outside the smoke become concealed|PC1 to creatures within it. This smoke can be dispersed by a strong wind.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.
