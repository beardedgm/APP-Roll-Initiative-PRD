# Clinging Shadows Stance

*uncommon, focus, manipulate, monk, shadow, stance*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Duration**: until you leave the stance
- **Source**: PC2 p.258

---

You adopt the stance of clinging shadows. You can make shadow grasp Strike|PC1|Strikes. These deal 1d4 void damage; are in the brawling group; and have the agile, grapple, reach, and unarmed traits.

While in clinging shadows stance, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Athletics|PC1 checks to Grapple|PC1, and to the DC for creatures to Escape|PC1 from you.
