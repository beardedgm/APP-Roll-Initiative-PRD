# Wall of Metal

*concentrate, manipulate, metal*

- **Level**: Spell 6
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: RoE p.145

---

You call forth a sheet of metal, forming a wall of iron, low-grade cold iron, or standard-grade silver up to 60 feet long, 30 feet high, and 1/4 inch thick. The wall doesn't need to stand vertically but must form a straight line in an unbroken open space so it doesn't pass through any creatures or objects, or the spell is lost. Each 10-foot-by-10- foot section of the wall has AC 10 and is immune to critical hits and precision damage. The wall's Hardness, HP, and BT use the statistics of a structure of the material you chose (see GM Core for material statistics). Creatures can move through a section of the wall that's broken.

---

**Heightened (7th)** The wall is high-grade silver or standard-grade dawnsilver.

**Heightened (8th)** The wall is standard-grade cold iron.

**Heightened (9th)** The wall is high-grade dawnsilver.

**Heightened (10th)** The wall is high-grade cold iron or standard-grade adamantine.
