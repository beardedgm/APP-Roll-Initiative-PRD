# Charged Javelin

*attack, electricity, evocation*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: 1 or more creatures
- **Source**: LOGM p.115

---

You fire a javelin of electricity that leaves a charged field around its target. Make a spell attack roll. The javelin deals 1d6 electricity damage and 1 persistent electricity damage.

**Critical Success** The javelin deals double damage, both initial and persistent.

**Success** The javelin deals full damage. As long as the target is taking persistent damage from this spell, creatures gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls with metal weapons or electricity effects against the target, and the target takes a –1 status penalty to saves against electricity effects.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The initial damage increases by 1d6, and the persistent damage increases by 1.
