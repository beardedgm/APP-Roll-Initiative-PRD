# Camel Spit

*acid, attack, concentrate, manipulate, morph*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Saving Throw**: AC
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: HotW p.84

---

You alter your stomach, esophagus, and tongue to be able to spit partially digested food with force. You can spit at a foe once you finish Casting the Spell and can repeat the attack once on each of your subsequent turns by taking a single action, which has the acid, attack, and concentrate traits. After your third spit attack, the spell ends. When you attack with camel spit, make a ranged spell attack roll against a creature within 15 feet, dealing 1d6 acid damage and causing the target to be dazzled|PC1 for 1 round if you hit. On a critical hit, you deal double damage and the target takes 1 persistent damage|PC1|persistent acid damage.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6, and the persistent damage|PC1 on a critical hit is increased by 1.
