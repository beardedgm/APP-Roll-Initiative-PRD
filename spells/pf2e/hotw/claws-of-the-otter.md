# Claws of the Otter

*cold, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: divine, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Source**: HotW p.84

---

Webbing grows between your fingers and your nails extend into vicious claws. For the spell's duration, you gain a +1 status bonus to Athletics|PC1 checks to Swim|PC1 and you gain a claws unarmed attack. They're an agile, finesse, unarmed attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage and an additional 1d6 cold damage.

---

**Heightened (+3)** The additional cold damage increases by 1d6.
