# Albatross Curse

*concentrate, manipulate, misfortune*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute (see text)
- **Source**: HotW p.84

---

You create a spectral albatross, a guiding bird for sailors, to hover around the target. You and allies within 30 feet of the target gain a +1 circumstance bonus to attacks against the target. The target creature can spend an action to Strike|PC1 the albatross, which automatically succeeds and kills it. The target must then attempt a Will save against your spell DC.
