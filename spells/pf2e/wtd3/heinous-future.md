# Heinous Future

*uncommon, curse, divination, incapacitation, mental, prediction*

- **Level**: Spell 6
- **Traditions**: divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (material, somatic)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: WtD3 p.26

---

You hold up a fractured mirror to the target, and in that mirror it sees a thing that may yet come to pass—a ghastly foretelling that the target instinctively knows is eerily possible. It's not a guaranteed thing, just a dreadful possibility, but it haunts the targets's thoughts all the same.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is slowed 1 and stupefied 1 for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is slowed 1 and stupefied 1 until it next gets 8 hours of rest.

**Critical Failure** The target is slowed 2 and stupefied 2, decreasing these conditions by 1 for each eight hours of rest it gets.
