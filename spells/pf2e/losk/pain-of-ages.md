# Pain of Ages

*uncommon, concentrate, manipulate, spirit*

- **Level**: Spell 6
- **Traditions**: divine
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 30-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: basic Will
- **Source**: LOSK p.99

---

You sense a spiritual resonance of deep anguish in the earth beneath your feet, the walls surrounding you, or the fragments of souls left behind in a place of calamity. You draw on this reservoir of pain and unleash its force against your enemies, dealing 8d8 spirit damage. On a critical failure, the creature is also stunned|PC1 for 1 round as it reels from the impact of so much anguish.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d8.
