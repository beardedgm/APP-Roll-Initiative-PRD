# Veil of Spirits

*uncommon, concentrate, manipulate, spirit*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Reflex
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: LOSK p.99

---

You draw on the essence of spiritual energy surrounding you to manifest a misty veil. When a creature that isn't immune to spirit damage begins its turn within the area, it must attempt a Reflex save. You can Dismiss|PC1 the veil.
