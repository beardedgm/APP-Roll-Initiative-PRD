# Split the Tongue

*uncommon, transmutation*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction (somatic)
- **Saving Throw**: basic Fortitude
- **Source**: AoE5 p.75

---

The deceiving creature's tongue splits like a serpent's tongue. The creature takes 1d8 slashing damage and must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is stupefied|CRB|stupefied 1 for 1d4 rounds.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature takes double damage and is stupefied|CRB|stupefied 2 for 1d4 rounds.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
