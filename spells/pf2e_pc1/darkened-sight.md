# Darkened Sight

*uncommon, cleric, concentrate, darkness, focus, manipulate*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.373

---

You infuse a creature's vision with darkness. It receives greater darkvision, allowing it to see even in magical darkness.
