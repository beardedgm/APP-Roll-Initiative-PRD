# Sleep

*concentrate, incapacitation, manipulate, mental, sleep*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: 5-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: PC1 p.357

---

Each creature in the area becomes drowsy, possibly nodding off. A creature that falls unconscious|PC1 from this spell doesn't fall prone|PC1 or release what it's holding. This spell doesn't prevent creatures from waking up due to a successful Perception|PC1 check, limiting its utility in combat.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes a –1 status penalty to Perception|PC1 checks for 1 round.

**Failure** The creature falls unconscious|PC1. If it's still unconscious|PC1 after 1 minute, it wakes up automatically.

**Critical Failure** The creature falls unconscious|PC1. If it's still unconscious|PC1 after 1 hour, it wakes up automatically.

---

**Heightened (4th)** The creatures fall unconscious|PC1 for 1 round on a failure or 1 minute on a critical failure. They fall prone|PC1 and release what they're holding, and they can't attempt Perception|PC1 checks to wake up. When the duration ends, the creature is sleeping normally instead of automatically waking up.
