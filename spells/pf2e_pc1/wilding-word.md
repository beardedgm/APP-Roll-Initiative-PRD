# Wilding Word

*uncommon, cantrip, hex, mental, witch*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.387

---

Your patron's majesty—or their displeasure—comes in a growl from your throat, making other creatures reluctant to harm you.

The target must attempt a Will save; if the creature is an animal, fungus, or plant, it takes a –1 circumstance penalty to its save.
