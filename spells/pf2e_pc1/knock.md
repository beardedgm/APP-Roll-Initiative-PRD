# Knock

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 door, lock, or container
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.340

---

You make the target easier to open. Knock grants a +4 status bonus to any creature that tries to open the target door, lock, or container with an Athletics|PC1 or a Thievery|PC1 check. You can attempt a Thievery|PC1 check to open the target as part of casting knock, and you add your level even if you're untrained.

Knock counteracts lock.
