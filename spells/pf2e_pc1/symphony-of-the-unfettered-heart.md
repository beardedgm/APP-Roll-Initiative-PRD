# Symphony of the Unfettered Heart

*uncommon, bard, composition, concentrate, focus*

- **Level**: Focus 5
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: you or 1 ally
- **Source**: PC1 p.371

---

Your symphony lifts listeners from their worldly concerns. Attempt a Performance|PC1 check to counteract an effect applying one of the following conditions to the target: grabbed|PC1, immobilized|PC1, paralyzed|PC1, restrained|PC1, slowed|PC1, or stunned|PC1. If you fail, you can't target the same effect on the target for 1 day.

Use the condition's source to determine the counteract DC (for example, the Escape|PC1 DC for grabbed|PC1).

---

**Heightened (9th)** You can target up to four creatures.
