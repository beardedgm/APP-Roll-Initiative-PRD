# Phantasmal Minion

*concentrate, manipulate, summon*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Source**: PC1 p.349

---

You summon a phantasmal minion|crb (see below). The minion is roughly the shape of a humanoid. You can choose to have it be invisible|PC1 or have an ephemeral appearance, but it's obviously a magical effect, not a real creature.
