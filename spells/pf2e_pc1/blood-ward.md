# Blood Ward

*uncommon, focus, hex, manipulate, witch*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.384

---

Your patron's aegis descends to shield a target from harm. Designate one of the following creature traits: aberration, animal. The target gains a +1 status bonus to its saving throws and AC against creatures with that trait.

---

**Heightened (5th)** The status bonus increases to +2.
