*uncommon, focus, hex, manipulate, mental, witch*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.386
