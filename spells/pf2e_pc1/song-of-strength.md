# Song of Strength

*uncommon, bard, cantrip, composition, concentrate, emotion, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Area**: 60-foot emanation
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Source**: PC1 p.371

---

You bolster your allies' physical strength with a hearty exhortation. You and your allies gain a +1 status bonus to Athletics|PC1 checks and to their DCs against Athletics|PC1 skill actions such as Disarm|PC1, Reposition|PC1, Shove|PC1, and Trip|PC1.
