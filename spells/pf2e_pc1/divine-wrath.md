# Divine Wrath

*concentrate, manipulate, sanctified, spirit*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: divine
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Source**: PC1 p.326

---

You channel the fury of divinity against your foes. You deal 4d10 spirit damage to enemies in the area, depending on a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is sickened 1|PC1.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes full damage and is sickened 2|PC1; while it is sickened|PC1, it is also slowed 1|PC1.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 4d10|4-10|1d10.
