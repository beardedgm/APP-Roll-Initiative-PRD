# Summon Dragon

*concentrate, manipulate, summon*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.360

---

You summon a creature that has the dragon trait and whose level is 5 to fight for you. If the dragon has a magical tradition trait (arcane, divine, occult, or primal), you can summon it only if you're using that tradition to cast summon dragon.

---

**Heightened (+1)** As listed on the summon trait.
