# Divine Immolation

*concentrate, fire, manipulate, sanctified, spirit*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: divine
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Reflex
- **Source**: PC1 p.325

---

Divine flames scour creatures within the area. Creatures take 6d6 fire damage and 2d6 persistent damage|PC1| persistent fire damage. The divine power within the flames scorches the spirit as well; a creature takes spirit damage instead of fire damage from divine immolation if that would be more detrimental to the creature (as determined by the GM).

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage and no persistent damage|PC1.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and persistent damage|PC1.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and double persistent damage|PC1.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 6d6|5-10|1d6 and persistent damage|PC1 increases by 2d6|5-10|1d6.
