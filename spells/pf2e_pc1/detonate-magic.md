# Detonate Magic

*uncommon, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 9
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: 1 magic item or spell effect
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Source**: PC1 p.324

---

You cause the magic within the target to dissipate in a destructive explosion. You attempt to counteracting the target. If the attempt succeeds, an explosion of magical force deals 8d6 force damage with a basic Reflex save. If you successfully counteract the magic of an item, it's deactivated for 1 week (or destroyed on a critical success) and the explosion is a 5-foot emanation from the item. If you successfully counteract a spell, the effect ends and the explosion affects either all creatures in the spells' area or the target of the spell and all creatures in a 5-foot emanation around it.
