# Earthworks

*uncommon, concentrate, earth, focus, manipulate, wizard*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: 1 to 3
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: 5-foot burst or more
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.388

---

With a ripple of earth, you raise small barriers from the ground. The ground in the area becomes difficult terrain. The spell's area is a 5-foot burst if you spent 1 action to cast it, a 10-foot burst if you spent 2 actions, or a 15-foot burst if you spent 3 actions. A creature can Interact|PC1 to clear the barriers from one 5-foot square adjacent to it.

---

**Heightened (4th)** You pull the barriers to float in the air, causing the spell to function as difficult terrain for flying creatures.
