# Entangling Flora

*concentrate, manipulate, plant, wood*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: all squares in a 20-foot burst that contain plants or fungi
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.329

---

Plants or fungi burst out or quickly grow, entangling creatures. The surfaces in the area counts as terrain. Each round that a creature starts its turn in the area, it must attempt a Reflex save. On a failure, it takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds until it leaves the area, and on a critical failure, it is also immobilized|PC1 for 1 round. Creatures can attempt to Escape|PC1 to remove these effects.
