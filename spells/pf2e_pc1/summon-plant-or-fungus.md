# Summon Plant Or Fungus

*concentrate, manipulate, summon*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.361

---

You summon a creature that has the plant or fungus trait and whose level is –1 to fight for you.

---

**Heightened (+1)** As listed on the summon trait.
