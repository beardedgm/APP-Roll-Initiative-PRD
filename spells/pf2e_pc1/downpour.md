# Downpour

*uncommon, cleric, concentrate, focus, manipulate, water*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 30-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.381

---

You call forth a torrential downpour, which extinguishes nonmagical flames. Creatures in the area are concealed|PC1 and gain fire resistance 10. Creatures outside the area are concealed|PC1 to those inside the area. If a creature with weakness to water ends its turn in the area, the downpour triggers its weakness.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The fire resistance increases by 2.
