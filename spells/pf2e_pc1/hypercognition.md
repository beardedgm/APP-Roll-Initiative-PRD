# Hypercognition

*concentrate*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: occult
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Source**: PC1 p.336

---

You rapidly catalog and collate information relevant to your current situation. You can instantly use up to 6 Recall Knowledge|PC1 actions as part of Casting this Spell. For these actions, you can't use any special abilities, reactions, or free actions that trigger when you Recall Knowledge|PC1.
