# Discern Secrets

*uncommon, cantrip, hex, manipulate, witch*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.386

---

Your patron deigns to whisper a few secrets. The target can Recall Knowledge|PC1, Seek|PC1, or Sense Motive|PC1 as a free action. The target gains a +1 status bonus to the statistic used for the roll (a skill or Perception|PC1) on the roll and as long as you Sustain|PC1 the spell. The target is temporarily immune to discern secrets for 1 minute.

---

**Heightened (5th)** You can target two creatures instead of one.
