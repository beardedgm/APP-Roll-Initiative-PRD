# Overstuff

*uncommon, cleric, concentrate, focus, manipulate*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 living creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude or Fortitude
- **Source**: PC1 p.376

---

Huge amounts of food and drink fill the target. It receives a full meal's worth of nourishment and must attempt a Fortitude save. A target sickened|PC1 by this spell takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed until it's no longer sickened|PC1.
