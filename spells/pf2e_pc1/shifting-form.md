# Shifting Form

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, morph, wizard*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.388

---

You change your body to better suit the moment. You gain one of the following abilities of your choice. You can Dismiss|PC1 this spell.

- You gain a 20-foot status bonus to your Speed.
- You gain a climb or swim Speed equal to half your Speed.
- You gain darkvision.
- You gain a pair of claws. These are agile finesse unarmed attacks that deal 1d8 slashing damage.
- You gain scent 60 feet (imprecise).
