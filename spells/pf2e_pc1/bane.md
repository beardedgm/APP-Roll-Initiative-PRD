# Bane

*aura, concentrate, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 10-foot emanation
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.317

---

You fill the minds of your enemies with doubt. Targets that fail their Will saves take a –1 status penalty to attack rolls as long as they are in the area. Once per round on subsequent turns, you can Sustain|PC1 the spell to increase the emanation's radius by 10 feet and force enemies in the area that weren't yet affected to attempt another saving throw. Bane can counteract bless|PC1.
