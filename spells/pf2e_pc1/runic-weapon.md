# Runic Weapon

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 weapon that is unattended or wielded by a willing creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.354

---

The weapon glimmers with magic and energy. The target becomes a +1 weapon potency|PC1|+1 striking|PC1 weapon, gaining a +1 item bonus to attack rolls and increasing the number of weapon damage dice to two.

---

**Heightened (6th)** The weapon is +2 weapon potency|PC1|+2 greater striking|PC1.

**Heightened (9th)** The weapon is +3 weapon potency|PC1|+3 major striking|PC1.
