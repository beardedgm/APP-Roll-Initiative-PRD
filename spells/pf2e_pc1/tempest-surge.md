# Tempest Surge

*uncommon, air, concentrate, druid, electricity, focus, manipulate*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Source**: PC1 p.382

---

You surround a foe in a swirling storm of violent winds, roiling clouds, and crackling lightning. The storm deals 1d12 electricity damage to the target with a basic Reflex save. On a failure, the target also is clumsy 2|PC1 for 1 round.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The initial damage increases by 1d12|1-10|1d12.
