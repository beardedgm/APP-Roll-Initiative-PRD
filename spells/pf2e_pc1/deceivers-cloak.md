# Deceiver's Cloak

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, hex, illusion, manipulate, witch*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Source**: PC1 p.384

---

Your patron drapes a cloak of illusion about you, making you appear as another creature of the same body shape with roughly similar height and weight as yourself. This has the effects of 3rd-rank illusory disguise|PC1.

---

**Heightened (6th)** You can appear as any creature of the same size, even with a completely different body shape.
