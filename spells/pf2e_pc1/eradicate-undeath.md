# Eradicate Undeath

*uncommon, cleric, concentrate, focus, manipulate, vitality*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 30-foot cone
- **Saving Throw**: basic Fortitude or Fortitude
- **Source**: PC1 p.374

---

A massive deluge of life energy causes the undead to fall apart. Each undead creature in the area takes 4d12 vitality damage.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d12.
