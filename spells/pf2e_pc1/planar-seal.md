# Planar Seal

*uncommon, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 7
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 60-foot burst
- **Duration**: until your next daily preparations
- **Source**: PC1 p.350

---

You create a visible magical barrier that attempts to counteract|crb|3|counteracting teleportation effects and planar travel into or out of the area, including items that allow access to extradimensional spaces. Planar seal tries to counteract any attempt to summon a creature into the area but doesn't stop the creature from departing when the summoning ends.
