# Fear

*concentrate, emotion, fear, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC1 p.331

---

You plant fear in the target; it must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is frightened 1|PC1.

**Failure** The target is frightened 2|PC1.

**Critical Failure** The target is frightened 3|PC1 and fleeing|PC1 for 1 round.

---

**Heightened (3rd)** You can target up to five creatures.
