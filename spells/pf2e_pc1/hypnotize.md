# Hypnotize

*illusion, manipulate, subtle, visual*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 10-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.336

---

You create a cloud of mesmerizing patterns and colors that hovers in the air. Creatures are dazzled|PC1 while inside the cloud. In addition, a creature must attempt a Will saving throw if it is inside the cloud when you cast it, when it enters the cloud, when it ends its turn within the cloud, or if it uses a Seek|PC1 or Interact|PC1 action on the cloud. A creature currently fascinated|PC1 by hypnotize doesn't attempt new saves.

**Success** The target is unaffected.

**Failure** The target is fascinated|PC1 by the cloud.

**Critical Failure** The target is fascinated|PC1 by the cloud. While it remains fascinated|PC1, it can't use reactions.
