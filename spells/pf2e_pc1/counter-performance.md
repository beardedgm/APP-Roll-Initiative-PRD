# Counter Performance

*uncommon, bard, composition, concentrate, focus, fortune, mental*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction
- **Area**: 60-foot emanation
- **Source**: PC1 p.370

---

Your performance protects you and your allies. Roll a Performance|PC1 check for a type you know—it must be an auditory performance if the trigger was auditory or a visual one for a visual trigger, and this action gains the traits for the type of performance. You and allies in the area can use the better result between your Performance|PC1 check and the saving throw.
