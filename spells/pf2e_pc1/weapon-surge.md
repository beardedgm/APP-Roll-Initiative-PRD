# Weapon Surge

*uncommon, cleric, focus, manipulate, sanctified*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 weapon you're wielding
- **Source**: PC1 p.381

---

Holding your weapon aloft, you fill it with divine energy. On your next Strike|PC1 with that weapon before the start of your next turn, you gain a +1 status bonus to the attack roll, the weapon deals an additional 1d6 spirit damage, and the Strike|PC1 gains the sanctified trait. Weapon surge ends once you complete this Strike|PC1 or the weapon leaves your possession.

---

**Heightened (5th)** The attack deals 2d6 additional spirit damage.

**Heightened (9th)** The attack deals 3d6 additional spirit damage.
