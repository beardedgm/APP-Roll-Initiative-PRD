# Howling Blizzard

*air, cold, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: 2 to 3
- **Area**: varies
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Source**: PC1 p.335

---

Freezing winds extend from your hands, pushing away from you with great force. If you Cast this Spell with 2 actions, it has an area of a 60-foot cone; if you Cast this Spell with 3 actions, it has a range of 500 feet and an area of a 30-foot burst. Each creature in the area takes 10d6 cold damage with a basic Reflex save. Snowdrifts and icy gales fill the area until the start of your next turn, making the area terrain.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 10d6|5-10|2d6.
