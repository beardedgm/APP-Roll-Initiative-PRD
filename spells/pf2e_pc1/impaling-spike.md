# Impaling Spike

*concentrate, manipulate, metal*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Reflex
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.221

---

You conjure a spike that thrusts up from the earth beneath a target creature, potentially impaling it. The spike is made of cold iron and deals 8d6 piercing damage. The target must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target dodges the spike and is unaffected.

**Success** The target is struck by the spike and takes half damage.

**Failure** The target is impaled through a leg or another non-vital body part. The creature takes full damage and, if it's standing on solid ground, becomes immobilized|PC1. It can attempt to Escape|PC1 (the DC is your spell DC). While it remains impaled, it takes damage from any weakness to cold iron it has at the end of each of its turns.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is impaled through a vital organ or its center of mass, taking double damage, and it is off-guard|PC1 as long as it's impaled.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 8d6|5-10|2d6.
