# Light

*cantrip, concentrate, light, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Duration**: until your next daily preparations
- **Source**: PC1 p.340

---

You create an orb of light that sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius (and dim light for the next 20 feet) in a color you choose. If you create the light in the same space as a willing creature, you can attach the light to the creature, causing it to float near that creature as it moves. You can Sustain|PC1 the spell to move the light up to 60 feet; you can attach or detach it from a creature as part of this movement.

You can Dismiss|PC1|Dismiss the spell. If you Cast the Spell while you already have four light spells active, you must choose one of the existing spells to end.

---

**Heightened (4th)** The orb sheds bright light in a 60-foot radius (and dim light for the next 60 feet).
