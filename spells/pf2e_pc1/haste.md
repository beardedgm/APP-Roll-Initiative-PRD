# Haste

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.335

---

Magic empowers the target to act faster. It gains the quickened|PC1 condition and can use the extra action each round only for Strike|PC1 and Stride|PC1 actions.

---

**Heightened (7th)** You can target up to 6 creatures.
