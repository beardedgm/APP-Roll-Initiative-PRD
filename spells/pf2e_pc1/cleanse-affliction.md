# Cleanse Affliction

*concentrate, healing, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 willing creature
- **Source**: PC1 p.320

---

Gentle restorative magic pushes back the effects of toxins and more complex maladies. Choose an afflictions on the target, such as a curse, disease, or poison. If it has advanced past stage one, reduce the stage by one. This reduction can be applied only once to a given case of an affliction, with the case ending when it's completely cured. Although the reduction can't occur again, heightened versions of this spell attempt to counteracting with each casting.

---

**Heightened (3rd)** Attempt to counteracting the affliction if it is a disease or poison.

**Heightened (4th)** Attempt to counteracting the affliction if it is a curse, disease or poison.
