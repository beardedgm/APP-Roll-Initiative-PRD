# Blood Vendetta

*curse*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: the triggering creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC1 p.318

---

You curse the target, punishing it for having the audacity to spill your blood. The target takes 2d6 persistent damage|PC1|persistent bleed damage and must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes half the persistent damage|PC1|persistent bleed damage.

**Failure** The target takes the full persistent damage|PC1|persistent bleed damage. Until the bleeding stops, the target has weakness 1 to piercing and slashing damage.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target takes double the persistent damage|PC1|persistent bleed damage.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The persistent damage|PC1|persistent bleed damage increases by 2d6.
