# Ignite Ambition

*uncommon, cleric, concentrate, emotion, focus, mental, subtle*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: one creature being influenced
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: PC1 p.372

---

You strengthen the target's ambition, increase its resentment of its allies, and make its allegiances more susceptible to change. It must attempt a Will save.
