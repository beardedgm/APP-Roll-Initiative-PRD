# Slither

*concentrate, manipulate, shadow*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: Reflex
- **Duration**: 1 minute (dismissible)
- **Source**: PC1 p.357

---

A mass of snakes made of shadow rise up to capture creatures in the area. Each creature in the area when you Cast the Spell takes 3d6 piercing damage and 1d6 persistent damage|PC1|persistent poison damage from a biting snake, and it's grabbed|PC1 or restrained|PC1 depending on its Reflex save. A creature that ends its turn in the area must also attempt this save, even if it's already grabbed|PC1 or restrained|PC1 by the snakes. You can dismiss|PC1 the spell.

**Success** The target is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is grabbed|PC1 by a snake. The snakes' Escape|PC1 DC is equal to your spell DC. A creature can attack a snake to release the creature. A snake's AC is equal to your spell DC, and it's destroyed if it takes 12 or more damage at once. New snakes continually regrow as long as the spell lasts, so destroying snakes doesn't prevent slither from capturing more creatures.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature takes double damage and is restrained|PC1 by a snake.
