# Whispering Quiet

*uncommon, cleric, focus, manipulate, sonic*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: 15-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.379

---

You suppress sound in an area, preventing anyone from giving away valuable secrets. This doesn't prevent those in the area from talking or Casting a Spell, but no creature more than 5 feet away can hear its voice without succeeding at a Perception|PC1 check against your spell DC, which might interfere with auditory or linguistic effects as well as communication. Other sounds created in the area are muffled to a similar volume unless that sound is created by an effect with the sonic trait.
