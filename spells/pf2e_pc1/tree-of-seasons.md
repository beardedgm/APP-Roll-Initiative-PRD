# Tree of Seasons

*concentrate, manipulate, plant, wood*

- **Level**: Spell 6
- **Traditions**: primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.364

---

You cause a Small tree to instantly sprout in an unoccupied space on the ground. Four seedpods grow from the tree, each filled with the magic of a different one of the four seasons. A creature can Interact to pluck one of the pods, and can then either throw it up to 30 feet as part of the same action or do so with a separate Interact|PC1 action later. When thrown, a pod explodes in a 5-foot burst, dealing 6d6 damage with a basic Reflex save against your spell DC. The damage type depends on the season of the pod: electricity for spring, fire for summer, poison for autumn, or cold for winter. When the spell ends, the tree withers away and any remaining pods rot, leaving behind non-magical seeds.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The burst's damage increases to 6d6|6-10|1d6.
