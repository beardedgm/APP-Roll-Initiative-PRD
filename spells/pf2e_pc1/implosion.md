# Implosion

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 9
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 corporeal creature
- **Saving Throw**: basic Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.338

---

You crush the target by causing it to collapse in on itself, dealing 75 damage. The first time you Sustain|PC1 the Spell, you must choose a new target to be subject to the same effect; the same creature can never be targeted more than once with a single casting of this spell. You also can't affect more than one creature per turn with implosion. You can't target a creature that's incorporeal, gaseous, or liquid, or one that otherwise lacks a solid form.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 75|9,10|10.
