# Rebuke Death

*uncommon, cleric, focus, healing, manipulate, vitality*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: 1 to 3
- **Area**: 20-foot emanation
- **Targets**: 1 living creature per action spent to Cast this Spell
- **Source**: PC1 p.376

---

You snatch creatures from the jaws of death, restoring them without the strain of a typical close call. You can spend 1 to 3 actions Casting this Spell, and you can target a number of creatures equal to the actions spent. Each target regains 3d6 Hit Points. If the target had the dying|PC1 condition, coming back from dying|PC1 due to this healing doesn't increase its wounded|PC1 condition.

---

**Heightened (+1)** Increase the healing by 1d6.
