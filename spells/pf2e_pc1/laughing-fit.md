# Laughing Fit

*concentrate, emotion, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 living creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: PC1 p.340

---

The target is overtaken with uncontrollable laughter. It must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is plagued with uncontrollable laugher. It can't use reactions.

**Failure** The target is slowed 1|PC1 and can't use reactions.

**Critical Failure** The target falls prone|PC1 and can't use actions or reactions for 1 round. It then suffers the failure effects.
