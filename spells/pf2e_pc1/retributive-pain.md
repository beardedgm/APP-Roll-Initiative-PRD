# Retributive Pain

*uncommon, cleric, focus, manipulate, mental, nonlethal*

- **Level**: Focus 4
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: the triggering creature
- **Saving Throw**: basic Fortitude or Fortitude
- **Source**: PC1 p.378

---

You vengefully reflect your pain upon your tormentor. The target takes mental damage equal to half the triggering damage.
