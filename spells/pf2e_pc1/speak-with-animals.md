# Speak with Animals

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Source**: PC1 p.358

---

You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the Diplomacy|PC1 skill with animals. The spell doesn't make them more friendly|PC1 than normal. Cunning animals are likely to be terse and evasive, while less intelligent ones often make inane comments.
