# Uplifting Overture

*uncommon, bard, cantrip, composition, concentrate, emotion, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: 1 ally
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Source**: PC1 p.372

---

Your performance makes allies feel they can succeed at anything. This counts as having prepared to Aid|PC1 your ally on a skill check of your choice. When you later use the Aid|PC1 reaction, you can roll Performance|PC1 instead of the normal skill check, and if you roll a failure, you get a success instead. If you are legendary in Performance|PC1, you automatically critically succeed.

The GM might rule that you can't use this ability if the act of encouraging your ally would interfere with the skill check (such as a check to Sneak|PC1 quietly or maintain a disguise).
