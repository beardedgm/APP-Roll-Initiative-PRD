# Face in the Crowd

*uncommon, cleric, focus, manipulate, visual*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.372

---

While in a crowd of roughly similar creatures, your appearance becomes bland and nondescript. You gain a +2 status bonus to Deception|PC1 and Stealth|PC1 checks to go incognito among the crowd, and you ignore difficult terrain caused by the crowd.

This counts as setting up a disguise for the Impersonate|PC1 use of Deception|PC1 and you add your level even if you're untrained.

---

**Heightened (3rd)** The spell gains a range of 10 feet and can target up to 10 creatures.
