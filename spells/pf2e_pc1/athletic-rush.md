# Athletic Rush

*uncommon, cleric, focus, manipulate*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Source**: PC1 p.377

---

Your body fills with physical power and skill. You gain a +10-foot status bonus to Speed and a +2 status bonus to Athletics|PC1 checks. As a part of Casting this Spell, you can use a Stride|PC1, Leap|PC1, Climb|PC1, or Swim|PC1 action. The spell's bonuses apply during that action.
