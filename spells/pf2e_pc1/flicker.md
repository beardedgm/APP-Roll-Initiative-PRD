# Flicker

*concentrate, manipulate, teleportation*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.332

---

You flicker quickly between your current plane and another. You gain resistance 5 to all damage, except force. At the end of each of your turns, you automatically teleport 10 feet in a random direction, as determined by the GM. You can Sustain|PC1 the spell to teleport in this way.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 3.
