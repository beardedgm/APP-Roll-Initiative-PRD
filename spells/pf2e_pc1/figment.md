# Figment

*cantrip, concentrate, illusion, manipulate, subtle*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Source**: PC1 p.331

---

You create a simple illusory sound or vision. A sound adds the auditory trait to the spell and the sound can't include intelligible words or elaborate music. A vision adds the visual trait, can be no larger than a 5-foot cube, and is clearly crude and undetailed if viewed from within 15 feet. When you Cast or Sustain|PC1 the Spell, you can attempt to Create a Diversion|PC1 with the illusion, gaining a +2 circumstance bonus to your Deception|PC1 check. If the attempt fails against a creature, that creature disbelieves the figment.
