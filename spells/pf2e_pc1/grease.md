# Grease

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: 4 contiguous 5-foot squares or
- **Targets**: 1 object of 1 Bulk or less
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.333

---

You conjure grease, choosing area or target.

- Area All solid ground in the area is covered with grease. Each creature standing on the greasy surface must succeed at a Reflex save or an Acrobatics|PC1 check against your spell DC or fall prone|PC1. Creatures using an action to move onto the greasy surface during the spell's duration must attempt either a Reflex save or an Acrobatics|PC1 check to Balance|PC1. A creature that Step|PC1|Steps or Crawls doesn't have to attempt a check or save.
- Target If you cast the spell on an unattended object, anyone trying to pick up the object must succeed at an Acrobatics|PC1 check or Reflex save against your spell DC to do so. If you target an attended object, the creature that has the object must attempt an Acrobatics|PC1 check or Reflex save. On a failure, the holder or wielder takes a –2 circumstance penalty to all checks that involve using the object; on a critical failure, the holder or wielder releases the item. The object lands in an adjacent square of the GM's choice. If you cast this spell on a worn object, the wearer gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves against attempts to grapple them.
