# Vision of Death

*concentrate, death, emotion, fear, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: 1 living creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Source**: PC1 p.366

---

You force the target to see a vision of its own death. It takes 8d6 mental damage with a Will save. If the target is reduced to 0 HP by this spell, its vision becomes reality and kills it instantly.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes half damage and is frightened 1|PC1.

**Failure** The target takes full damage and is frightened 2|PC1.

**Critical Failure** The target double damage, is frightened 4|PC1 and is fleeing|PC1 for as long as it is frightened|PC1.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 8d6|4-10|2d6.
