# Unfettered Movement

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature touched
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: PC1 p.365

---

You repel hindrances that would affect a creature. While under this spell's effect, the target ignores effects that would give them a circumstance penalty to Speed. When they attempt to Escape|PC1 an effect that has them immobilized|PC1, grabbed|PC1, or restrained|PC1, they automatically succeed unless the effect is magical and of a higher rank than the unfettered movement spell.
