# Dispel Magic

*concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: 1 spell effect or unattended magic item
- **Source**: PC1 p.325

---

You unravel the magic behind a spell or effect. Attempt a counteracting check against the target. If you succeed against a spell effect, you counteract it. If you succeed against a magic item, the item becomes a mundane item of its type for 10 minutes. This doesn't change the item's non-magical properties. If the target is an artifact or similar item, you automatically fail.
