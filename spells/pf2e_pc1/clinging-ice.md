# Clinging Ice

*uncommon, cantrip, cold, hex, manipulate, witch*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Reflex
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.386

---

Freezing sleet and heavy snowfall collect on the target's feet and legs, dealing 1d4 cold damage and other effects depending on its Reflex save.
