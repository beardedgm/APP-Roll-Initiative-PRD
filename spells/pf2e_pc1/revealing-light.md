# Revealing Light

*concentrate, light, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: 10-foot burst
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC1 p.353

---

A wave of magical light washes over the area. You choose the appearance of the light, such as colorful, heatless flames or sparkling motes. A creature affected by revealing light is dazzled|PC1. If the creature was invisible|PC1, it becomes concealed|PC1 instead. If the creature was already concealed|PC1 for any other reason, it is no longer concealed.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The light affects the creature for 2 rounds.

**Failure** The light affects the creature for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The light affects the creature for 10 minutes.
