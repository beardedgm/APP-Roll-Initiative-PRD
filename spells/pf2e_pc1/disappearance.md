# Disappearance

*illusion, manipulate, subtle*

- **Level**: Spell 8
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: PC1 p.324

---

You shroud a creature from others' senses. The target becomes undetected|PC1, not just to sight but to all senses, allowing the target to count as invisible|PC1 no matter what precise and imprecise senses an observer might have. It's still possible for a creature to find the target by Seek|PC1|Seeking, looking for disturbed dust, hearing gaps in the sound spectrum, or finding some other way to discover the presence of an otherwise-undetectable creature.
