# Blur

*illusion, visual*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC1 p.318

---

The target's form appears blurry. It becomes concealed|PC1. As the nature of this effect still leaves the target's location obvious, the target can't use this concealment to Hide|PC1 or Sneak|PC1.
