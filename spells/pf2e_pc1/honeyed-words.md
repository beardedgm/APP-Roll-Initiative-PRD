# Honeyed Words

*concentrate, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: PC1 p.335

---

Falsehoods pass your lips as smoothly as silk. You gain a +4 status bonus to Deception|PC1 checks to Lie|PC1 and against Perception|PC1 checks to discern if you are telling the truth, and you add your level even if you're untrained. If the implausibility of your lies prompts a circumstance penalty or a DC increase, reduce that penalty or increase by half.
