# Fireball

*evocation, fire*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 500 feet
- **Area**: 20-foot burst
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Source**: CRB p.338

---

A roaring blast of fire appears at a spot you designate, dealing 6d6 fire damage.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
