# Burning Hands

*evocation, fire*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Area**: 15-foot cone
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Source**: CRB p.322

---

Gouts of flame rush from your hands. You deal 2d6 fire damage to creatures in the area.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
