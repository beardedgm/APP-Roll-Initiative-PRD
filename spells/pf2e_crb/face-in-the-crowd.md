# Face in the Crowd

*uncommon, cleric, illusion, visual*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆ (somatic)
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: CRB p.392

---

While in a crowd of roughly similar creatures, your appearance becomes bland and nondescript. You gain a +2 status bonus to Deception and Stealth checks to go unnoticed among the crowd, and you ignore terrain caused by the crowd.

---

**Heightened (3rd)** The spell gains a range of 10 feet and can target up to 10 creatures.
