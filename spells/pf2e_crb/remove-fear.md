# Remove Fear

*enchantment*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: divine, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Source**: CRB p.363

---

With a touch, you ease a creature's fears. You can attempt to counteract a single fear effect that the target suffers from.

This frees only the target, not any other creatures under the fear effect.

---

**Heightened (6th)** The spell's range increases to 30 feet, and you can target up to 10 creatures.
