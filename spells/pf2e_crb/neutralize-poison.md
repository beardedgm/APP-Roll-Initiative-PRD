# Neutralize Poison

*healing, necromancy*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: divine, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Source**: CRB p.354

---

You pour healing magic through the target in an attempt to cure one poison afflicting it. Attempt a counteract check against the poison.
