# Acid Arrow

*acid, attack, evocation*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Source**: CRB p.316

---

You conjure an arrow of acid that continues corroding the target after it hits. Make a spell attack against the target.

On a hit, you deal 3d8 acid damage plus 1d6 persistent acid damage. On a critical hit, double the initial damage, but not the persistent damage.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The initial damage increases by 3d8|4,6,8,10|2d8, and the persistent acid damage increases by 1d6|4,6,8,10|1d6.
