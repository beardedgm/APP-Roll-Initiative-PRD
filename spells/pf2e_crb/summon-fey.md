# Summon Fey

*conjuration*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆ (material, somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: CRB p.375

---

You conjure a fey to fight for you. This works like summon animal, except you summon a common creature that has the fey trait and whose level is Creature Type=Fey.
