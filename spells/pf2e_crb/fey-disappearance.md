# Fey Disappearance

*uncommon, enchantment, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆ (somatic)
- **Duration**: until the end of your next turn
- **Source**: CRB p.404

---

You become invisible and ignore natural terrain (such as underbrush). Any hostile action you use ends this invisibility, but you still ignore natural terrain.

---

**Heightened (5th)** If you use a hostile action, the invisibility doesn't end.
