# Discern Lies

*uncommon, divination, mental, revelation*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: CRB p.330

---

Falsehoods ring in your ears like discordant notes. You gain a +4 status bonus to Perception checks when someone Lies.
