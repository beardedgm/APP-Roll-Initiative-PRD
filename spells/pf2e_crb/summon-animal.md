# Summon Animal

*conjuration*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆ (material, somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: CRB p.375

---

You conjure an animal to fight for you. You summon a common creature that has the animal trait and whose level is Creature Type=Animal.

Heightening the spell increases the maximum level of creature you can summon.
