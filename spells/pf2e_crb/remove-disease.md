# Remove Disease

*healing, necromancy*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: divine, primal
- **Cast**: 10 minutes (material, somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Source**: CRB p.363

---

Healing magic purges disease from a creature's body. You attempt to counteract one disease afflicting the target.
