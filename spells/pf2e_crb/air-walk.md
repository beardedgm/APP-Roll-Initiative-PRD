# Air Walk

*air, transmutation*

- **Level**: Spell 4
- **Traditions**: divine, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 5 minutes
- **Source**: CRB p.317

---

The target can walk on air as if it were solid ground. It can ascend and descend in this way at a maximum of a 45-degree angle.
