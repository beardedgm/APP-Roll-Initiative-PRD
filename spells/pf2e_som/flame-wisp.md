# Flame Wisp

*evocation, fire*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: SoM p.106

---

Three faintly glowing wisps of fire float around your head.

Each time you hit a creature with a Strike, one of the wisps goes hurtling towards that creature, dealing 1d4 fire damage. If you Cast a Spell with the fire trait while you have fewer than three wisps, a new wisp appears.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d4.
