# Nature's Reprisal

*plant, poison, transmutation, wood*

- **Level**: Spell 6
- **Traditions**: primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆ (material, somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 120 feet
- **Area**: all squares on the ground that contain plants in an 80-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: SoM p.117

---

The plant life within the area begins to writhe and lash out against your enemies as you call upon nature to impede your foes. To your enemies, the area becomes terrain, and areas that were naturally terrain due to plants become terrain as well as hazardous terrain, dealing 6 poison damage to an enemy each time it enters an affected square.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The poison damage of the hazardous terrain increases by 1.
