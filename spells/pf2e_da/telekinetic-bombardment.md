# Telekinetic Bombardment

*evocation*

- **Level**: Spell 7
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 500 feet
- **Area**: 10-foot burst or 30-foot line
- **Saving Throw**: Reflex
- **Source**: DA p.107

---

You grasp a piece of the landscape—such as a tree, carriage, or piece of masonry—and lob it in your foe's direction. Your missile crashes down in either a 10-foot burst (for most missiles) or a 30-foot line (for something long and tall, like a tree or a ship's mast), dealing 14d6 bludgeoning damage and turning its area into terrain as it breaks into rubble. All creatures in the area must attempt a Reflex save. If the area is a line, it doesn't have to start from your square but can instead start anywhere in range, as long as the entire area remains within range.
