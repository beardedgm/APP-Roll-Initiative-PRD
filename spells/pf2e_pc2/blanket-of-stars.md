# Blanket of Stars

*concentrate, illusion, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 6
- **Traditions**: occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 10 minutes
- **Source**: PC2 p.241

---

A flowing cloak of utter darkness drapes over you, filled with pinpricks of light like distant stars. It imparts the stillness of the cosmos to you, granting you a +2 status bonus to Stealth|PC1 checks to Hide|PC1 and Sneak|PC1. While outside under a starry night sky, you're also invisible|PC1 as long as you remain still. When moving under a starry night sky, you're concealed|PC1 instead.

Gazing too closely into the stars is disorienting. Any creature that ends its turn adjacent to you must attempt a Will save; this is a mental, visual effect.
