# Soul Siphon

*uncommon, focus, manipulate, oracle, void*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 living creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Source**: PC2 p.259

---

You siphon off a portion of the target's soul to bolster your own vitality. The target must attempt a Fortitude save or take 1d4 void damage. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to the total amount of HP the target loses, including the HP lost from becoming drained|PC1.
