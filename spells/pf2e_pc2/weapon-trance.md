# Weapon Trance

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, oracle*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Duration**: sustained up to 1 minute; see text
- **Source**: PC2 p.259

---

The serenity of violence fills your mind, giving you a heightened sense of knowing exactly where your weapons need to be. For the duration, your proficiency with martial weapons is equal to your proficiency with simple weapons. You automatically Sustain|PC1 this spell as a free action the first time you hit with a weapon Strike|PC1 each round.
