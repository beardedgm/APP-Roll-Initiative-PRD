# Hero's Defiance

*uncommon, champion, concentrate, focus, healing, vitality*

- **Level**: Focus 10
- **Cast**: ◇ Free Action
- **Source**: PC2 p.257

---

You shout in defiance and are filled with a burst of healing. Just before applying the attack's damage, you recover 6d8+20 Hit Points. If this is enough to prevent the attack from bringing you to 0 Hit Points, you don't become unconscious|PC1 or dying|PC1. Either way, cheating death is difficult, and you can't use hero's defiance again until you Refocus|PC1 or make your daily preparations. Hero's defiance can't be used against effects with the death trait or that would leave no remains, such as disintegrate|PC1.
