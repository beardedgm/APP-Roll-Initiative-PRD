# Interstellar Void

*uncommon, cold, concentrate, focus, manipulate, oracle*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.260

---

You call upon the frigid depths of outer space to bring a terrible chill to your enemy. The target is cloaked in the freezing void, which deals 3d6 cold damage immediately and the first time each round you Sustain|PC1 the spell on future turns, with a basic Fortitude save. The target is fatigued|PC1 for as long as the effect persists.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The cold damage increases by 1d6.
