# Cloak of Colors

*concentrate, manipulate, visual*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.242

---

A cloak of swirling colors shrouds the target. Creatures are dazzled|PC1 while adjacent to it, and attacking the target causes a brilliant flash of light. A creature that hits the target with a melee attack must attempt a Will save. The creature is then temporarily immune until the end of its turn; this effect has the incapacitation trait.
