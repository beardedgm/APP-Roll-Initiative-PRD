# Spout

*cantrip, concentrate, manipulate, water*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: a 5-foot cube
- **Saving Throw**: basic Reflex
- **Source**: PC2 p.252

---

Water blasts upward, coming out of the ground, rising from a pool, or even manifesting from thin air. Any creatures in the area take 2d4 bludgeoning damage, with a basic Reflex saving throw. A creature that critically fails its save is disoriented by the explosion of water, becoming off-guard|PC1 until the end of your next turn.

You can change this spell's area to a 5-foot burst, provided you center the burst in a body of water. This body of water can be as small as a pond or creek, but not as small as a puddle or bathtub.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
