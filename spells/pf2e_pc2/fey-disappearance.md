# Fey Disappearance

*uncommon, focus, manipulate, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Source**: PC2 p.264

---

You become invisible|PC1 and ignore natural difficult terrain (such as underbrush). Any hostile|PC1 action you use ends this invisibility, but you still ignore natural difficult terrain.

---

**Heightened (5th)** If you use a hostile|PC1 action, the invisibility doesn't end.
