# Elemental Toss

*uncommon, attack, focus, manipulate, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: AC
- **Source**: PC2 p.264

---

With a flick of your wrist, you fling a chunk of your elemental matter at your foe. Make a ranged spell attack roll, dealing 1d8 damage on a success and double damage on a critical success.

This spell deals damage according to your elemental influence (for instance, slashing for air or fire for fire) and has your element's trait.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
