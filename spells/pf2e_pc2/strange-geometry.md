# Strange Geometry

*concentrate, illusion, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 5
- **Traditions**: occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: 4 cubes, each 10 feet on a side
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.252

---

You cause the areas to appear to swell, bend, and break, twisting together in a bizarre spatial geometry. The cubes of the spell's area can't be adjacent to one another. A creature must attempt a Will save if it's in one of the cubes when you Cast the Spell, or if it later enters one of the areas, with the following effects.

A creature interacting with the illusion can also attempt a Will save to disbelieve the illusion, as normal.
