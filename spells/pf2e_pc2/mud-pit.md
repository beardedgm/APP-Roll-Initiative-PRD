# Mud Pit

*concentrate, earth, manipulate, water*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Area**: squares on the ground in a 15-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.248

---

Thick, clinging mud covers the ground, 1 foot deep. The mud is difficult terrain.
