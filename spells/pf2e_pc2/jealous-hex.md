# Jealous Hex

*uncommon, concentrate, curse, focus, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.264

---

You draw forth a hag's innate jealousy to deny a target its greatest attribute. The target is cursed with an adverse condition depending on its highest attribute modifier: Strength (enfeebled|PC1); Dexterity (clumsy|PC1); Constitution (drained|PC1); or Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (stupefied|PC1). On a tie, the creature decides which of the conditions associated with the tied ability attribute to take. The target must attempt a Will save. At the start of each of your turns, the target can attempt another Will save, ending the effect on a success.
