# Telepathic Demand

*concentrate, incapacitation, linguistic, manipulate, mental*

- **Level**: Spell 9
- **Traditions**: arcane, divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Targets**: 1 creature you've telepathically contacted before
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: varies
- **Source**: PC2 p.253

---

You send the target a message of 25 words or fewer, and it can respond immediately with its own message of 25 words or fewer. Your message is insidious and has the effect of a suggestion|PC1 spell, with the message substituting for the spoken suggestion. On a successful save, the target is temporarily immune for 1 day, and on a critical success, the target is temporarily immune for 1 month. You can target a creature only if you've previously been in telepathic contact with it before, such as via the telepathy|PC1 spell.
