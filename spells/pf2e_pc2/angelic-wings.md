# Angelic Wings

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, light, manipulate, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Duration**: 3 rounds
- **Source**: PC2 p.262

---

Wings of pure light spread out from your back, granting you a fly Speed equal to your Speed. Your wings cast bright light in a 30-foot radius. When this spell's duration would end, if you're still flying, you float to the ground, as gentle landing|PC1.

---

**Heightened (5th)** The duration increases to 1 minute.
