# Invisible Item

*concentrate, illusion, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 object
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Source**: PC2 p.247

---

You make the object invisible|PC1. This makes it undetected|PC1 to all creatures, though the creatures can attempt to find the target, making it hidden|PC1 to them instead if they succeed. If the item is used as part of a hostile|PC1 action, the spell ends after that hostile|PC1 action is completed. Making a weapon invisible|PC1 typically doesn't give any advantage to the attack, except that an invisible|PC1 thrown weapon or piece of ammunition can be used for an attack without necessarily giving information about the attacker's hiding place unless the weapon returns to the attacker.

---

**Heightened (3rd)** The duration is until the next time you make your daily preparations.

**Heightened (7th)** The duration is unlimited.
