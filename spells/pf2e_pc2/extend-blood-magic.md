# Extend Blood Magic

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, spellshape, sorcerer*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Source**: PC2 p.265

---

You call upon the arcane energy coursing through your blood to extend the magic it grants. If your next action is to Cast a Spell that grants you a blood magic effect that lasts for at least 1 round, you or a target gain the blood magic effect for an additional round.

You can have only one extended blood magic effect at a time.
