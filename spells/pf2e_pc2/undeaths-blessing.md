# Undeath's Blessing

*uncommon, focus, manipulate, sorcerer, void*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 living creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: PC2 p.265

---

You instill within a creature the touch of the grave. For the duration, harm|PC1 and heal|PC1 spells treat the creature as undead. In addition, harm|PC1 spells gain a +2 status bonus to the Hit Points restored to the target. An unwilling target can attempt a Will save to reduce the effects.

**Heightened (+1)** The status bonus to the Hit Points restored increases by 2.
