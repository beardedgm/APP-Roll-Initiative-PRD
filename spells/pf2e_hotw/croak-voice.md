# Croak Voice

*concentrate, curse, manipulate, morph*

- **Level**: Spell 3
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: HotW p.84
