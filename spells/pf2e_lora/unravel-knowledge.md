# Unravel Knowledge

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, hex, manipulate, mental, witch*

- **Level**: Focus 3
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: LORA p.45

---

Your patron reaches into the target's mind, plucking out their past training like pulling a thread from a cloth. Choose a single action, activity, or spell you know the target can use, such as from seeing it previously or identifying the creature's capabilities with a Recall Knowledge check. You could select, for example, the Strike action, the Disarm action, a dragon's breath, or the haste|PC1 spell if you were aware the target could use them. The target attempts a Will save.
