# Coiling Dance

*rare, concentrate, holy, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: divine, occult
- **Cast**: ◆◆
- **Area**: 30-foot emanation
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: LORA p.109

---

You perform a dance that seeks to pass on the knowledge and wisdom of a naga. Your allies in the area are filled with sacred energy, making their spells and attacks holy. Creatures or effects that would be unholy don't gain this benefit.

When you cast or Sustain|PC1 this spell, you can choose an ally in the area that's grabbed|PC1, immobilized|PC1, or restrained|PC1. They can immediately use a reaction to Escape|PC1; they can use your Occultism|PC1 or Religion|PC1 modifier for the check instead of their unarmed attack, Acrobatics|PC1, or Athletics|PC1 modifier if that would be better.
