# Cutting Eye

*uncommon, concentrate, focus, wizard*

- **Level**: Focus 1
- **Cast**: ◈ Reaction
- **Source**: LORA p.117

---

Your eyes open enviously, stealing the power of an opponent for yourself. You are not off-guard|PC1 to the attacker or spellcaster until the beginning of your next turn. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your AC against the triggering attack or a +2 circumstance bonus to the triggering save.
