# Information Overload

*rare, cantrip, concentrate, hex, mental, witch*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Cast**: ◆
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Saving Throw**: Will
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: LORA p.44

---

You call on Cobyslarni to open the target's mind to knowledge—too much knowledge. The target's thoughts flood with irrelevant and confusing information, causing it to become stupefied|PC1|stupefied 1 if it fails a Will save (or stupefied|PC1|stupefied 2 on a critical failure).
