# The Queen's Rainbow

*uncommon, auditory, concentrate, manipulate*

- **Level**: Spell 2
- **Traditions**: arcane, occult, primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆◆
- **Area**: 60-foot line
- **Saving Throw**: Fortitude
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: LORA p.71

---

You tell the story of the Queen of Bees and her retinue trying to pass through a rainbow on their way to visit the King of Spiders. You conjure forth a large, transparent rainbow. Creatures who enter or begin their turn in the rainbow's space must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or become dazzled|PC1 for 1 round (or blinded|PC1 for 1 round on a critical failure).
