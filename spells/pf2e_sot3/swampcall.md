# Swampcall

*uncommon, earth, transmutation*

- **Level**: Spell 1
- **Traditions**: primal
- **Cast**: ◆◆ (somatic, verbal)
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Area**: 10-foot burst
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Source**: SoT3 p.77

---

You call upon the spirits of the soil to twist and churn, transforming the terrain in the targeted area into a sodden mess. The area becomes terrain. Creatures in the area when you Cast this Spell must attempt a Reflex saving throw.
