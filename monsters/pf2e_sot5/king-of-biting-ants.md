**Counterspell** ◈ **Trigger** A creature Casts a Spell the King of Biting Ants knows; The King of Biting Ants expends a spell slot to counter the triggering creature's casting. **Discorporate When the King of Biting** Ants is reduced to 0 HP, he discorporates, and the component ants that make up his body disperse in every direction. If even a single ant escapes, the King of Biting Ants will eventually reform using a process that typically takes 1d10 days. Applying area damage immediately after the King of Biting Ants is killed prevents his return.
