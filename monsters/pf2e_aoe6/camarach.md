# Camarach
*Creature 17*

uncommon, cn, gargantuan, monitor

---

**Perception** +27
**Languages** abyssal, celestial, infernal, protean
**STR** +10, **DEX** +5, **CON** +6, **INT** +6, **WIS** +6, **CHA** +9

**Thoughtsense** (divination, mental, occult) The camarach senses the presence of non-mindless creatures within 120 feet as an imprecise sense.

---

**AC** 40
**Fort** +31, **Ref** +26, **Will** +29
**HP** 320
**Resistances** physical 15
**Weaknesses** lawful 15

---

Speed fly 60 feet, swim 60 feet

**Melee** jaws +35 (magical, reach <20 feet>); **Damage** 3d10+18 piercing plus Improved Grab plus Improved Grab

**Innate occult Spells** (DC 43)

**Disgorge Portal** ◆◆ (evocation, light, occult) The camarach vomits a spray of coruscating light. This has the effect of a DC 42 prismatic spray, except that each creature is affected by one violet beam and one other beam (roll 1d6 instead of 1d8 to determine which other beam). The camarach can't Disgorge a Portal again for 1d4 rounds.
**Exile** ◆ (conjuration, occult, teleportation) The camarach shunts one creature within its body through an inner portal to send it away. The target must attempt a DC 38 Will save. **Critical Success** The creature is teleported to an open space it chooses within 30 feet of the camarach.
**Success** The creature isn't teleported.
**Failure** The creature is teleported to a random open space 1 mile from the camarach.
**Critical Failure** The creature is teleported to a random other plane, as plane shift.
**Fast Swallow** ◈ **Trigger** The camarach Grabs a creature; The camarach uses Swallow Whole.
**Project False Image** When the camarach casts project image, its illusory image can be another creature of its size or a structure of its size that features a large portal (such as a freestanding archway or a large hut with an open doorway).
**Swallow Whole** ◆ Huge, 3d10+9 bludgeoning, Rupture 34.
