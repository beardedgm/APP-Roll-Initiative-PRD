**Hampering Shot** ◆◆ The Zephyr Guard makes a heavy crossbow Strike that deals one additional weapon damage die. If the Strike hits, the target takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speeds and is slowed|CRB|slowed 1. These last for 1 round, or 2 rounds if the Strike was a critical success.

**Subduing Strikes** A Zephyr Guard takes no penalty for making nonlethal attacks with a weapon that doesn't have the nonlethal trait. The guard's nonlethal Strikes against a creature that is not wielding a weapon deal an additional 2d6 damage.
