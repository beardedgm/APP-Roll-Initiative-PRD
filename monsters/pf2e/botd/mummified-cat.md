# Mummified Cat
*Creature 0*

ne, tiny, mummy, undead

---

**Perception** +7; darkvision, scent 30 feet (precise)

**Skills** Acrobatics +4, Athletics +5, Stealth +9

**Recall Knowledge - Undead** (Religion): DC 14

**STR** +1, **DEX** +0, **CON** +2, **INT** -4, **WIS** +3, **CHA** -2

---

**AC** 15

**Fort** +8, **Ref** +4, **Will** +7

**HP** 17; negative healing

**Immunities** death effects, disease, paralyzed, poison, unconscious

Speed 25 feet, climb 15 feet

**Melee** ◆ claw +8/+4/+0 (agile, reach <0 feet>); **Damage** 1d4+1 piercing

**Innate divine Spells** (DC 15)

  **1st** fear

**Pounce** ◆ The mummified cat Strides and makes a Strike at the end of its movement. If the mummified cat began this action hidden, it remains hidden until after this ability's Strike.

**Sneak Attack** The mummified cat deals an additional 1d4 precision damage to flat-footed creatures.
