**Ranged** ◆ crossbow +10/+5/+0 (range increment <120 feet>, reload <1>); **Damage** 1d8 piercing

**Cheek Pouches** The ratfolk has stretchy cheek pouches that can store up to 1 cubic foot of objects (though no more than 4 light items). The ratfolk can remove or store an item using the Interact action. As long as the ratfolk has at least one object in its cheek pouches, its speech is noticeably difficult to understand.

**Katar Specialist** The smugglers have trained extensively with the katar blade. Any katar they wield gains the finesse trait.
