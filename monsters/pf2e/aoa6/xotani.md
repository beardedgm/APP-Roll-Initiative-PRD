*Creature 20*

unique, ce, gargantuan, beast, fire

**Perception** +39; blindsight 120 feet (precise), darkvision

**Languages** aklo (can't speak any language)

**Skills** Athletics +41

**Recall Knowledge - Beast** (Arcana, Nature): DC 50

**STR** +10, **DEX** +8, **CON** +9, **INT** -4, **WIS** +7, **CHA** +6

---

**AC** 48

**Fort** +36, **Ref** +33, **Will** +30

**HP** 385; regeneration 25

**Immunities** clumsy, disease, drained, enfeebled, fire, mental, paralyzed, petrified, poison, polymorph, stupefied

**Resistances** electricity 22, physical 22, sonic 22

**Weaknesses** cold 22

**Frightful Presence** (aura, emotion, fear, mental) 300 feet, DC 39

**Light Blindness** Xotani's light blindness is caused by only sunlight. Reactive Xotani gains 3 reactions each round. It can still use only one reaction per trigger.

**Attack of Opportunity** ◈

**Firebleed** ◈ (fire) **Trigger** Xotani is damaged; Xotani's wound erupts with magma in a 30-foot cone, dealing 6d8 fire damage and 6d8 persistent fire damage (DC 42 basic Reflex save).

Speed 60 feet, burrow 40 feet, climb 60 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +38/+33/+28 (reach <20 feet>); **Damage** 5d10+8 piercing plus 2d10 persistent fire

**Melee** ◆ claw +38/+34/+30 (agile, reach <20 feet>); **Damage** 5d8+8 slashing plus 2d8 persistent fire

**Ranged** ◆ lava bomb +38/+33/+28 (range increment <80 feet>); **Damage** 2d10+8 bludgeoning plus 2d10 persistent fire

**Bomb Barrage** ◆◆ Xotani makes three lava bomb Strikes. These Strikes can be made against the same creature or different creatures, as long as all targets are within 40 feet of each other.

**Breath Weapon** ◆◆ (arcane, evocation, fire) Xotani breathes a blast of flame that deals 18d6 fire damage plus 4d6 persistent fire damage to all creatures in a 70-foot cone (DC 42 basic Reflex save). It can't use Breath Weapon again for 1d4 rounds.

**Swallow Whole** ◆ (attack) Huge, 8d6+8 bludgeoning plus 12d6 fire, Rupture 35

**Trample** ◆◆◆ Huge or smaller, claw, DC 42. When Xotani Tramples, it can Stride up to triple its Speed.
