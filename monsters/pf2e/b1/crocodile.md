# Crocodile
*Creature 2*

n, large, animal

---

**Perception** +7; low-light vision

**Skills** Athletics +8, Stealth +7

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 16

**STR** +4, **DEX** +1, **CON** +3, **INT** -5, **WIS** +1, **CHA** -4

---

**AC** 18

**Fort** +9, **Ref** +7, **Will** +5

**HP** 30

---

Speed 20 feet, swim 25 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +10/+5/+0; **Damage** 1d10+4 piercing plus Grab plus Grab

**Melee** ◆ tail +10/+6/+2 (agile); **Damage** 1d6+4 bludgeoning

**Aquatic Ambush** ◆ 35 feet

**Death Roll** ◆ (attack) **Requirements** The crocodile must have a creature grabbed.; The crocodile tucks its legs and rolls rapidly, twisting its victim. It makes a jaws Strike with a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll against the grabbed creature. If it hits, it also knocks the creature prone. If it fails, it releases the creature.

**Deep Breath** The crocodile can hold its breath for about 2 hours.
