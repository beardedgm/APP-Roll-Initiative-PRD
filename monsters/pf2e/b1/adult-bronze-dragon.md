# Adult Bronze Dragon
*Creature 13*

lg, huge, amphibious, dragon, water

---

**Perception** +23; darkvision, scent 60 feet (imprecise)

**Languages** aquan, common, draconic, dwarven, elven, gnomish; speak with animals

**Skills** Acrobatics +22, Arcana +28, Athletics +24, Diplomacy +23, Intimidation +23, Occultism +24, Stealth +22

**Recall Knowledge - Dragon** (Arcana): DC 31

**STR** +7, **DEX** +3, **CON** +4, **INT** +5, **WIS** +4, **CHA** +4

---

**AC** 34

**Fort** +24, **Ref** +23, **Will** +26; +1 status to all saves vs. magic

**HP** 260

**Immunities** electricity, paralyzed, sleep

**Electricity Aura** (aura, electricity) 10 feet, 1d12 electricity damage. The bronze dragon can turn this aura on or off using a single action, which has the concentrate trait, and it can choose to not affect allies with the aura.

**Frightful Presence** (aura, emotion, fear, mental) 90 feet, DC 31

**Attack of Opportunity** ◈ Jaws only.

---

Speed 40 feet, fly 140 feet, swim 50 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +28/+23/+18 (electricity, magical, reach <15 feet>); **Damage** 2d12+15 piercing plus 1d12 electricity

**Melee** ◆ claw +28/+24/+20 (agile, magical, reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 2d10+15 slashing

**Melee** ◆ tail +26/+21/+16 (magical, reach <15 feet>); **Damage** 1d10+13 bludgeoning

**Innate arcane Spells** (DC 33)

  **Constant (2nd)** speak with animals

  **2nd** obscuring mist (at will)

**Breath Weapon** ◆◆ The bronze dragon breathes in one of two ways. The dragon can't use Breath Weapon again for 1d4 rounds. - Lightning (arcane, electricity, evocation); The dragon breathes lightning in a 80-foot line that deals 8d12 electricity damage (DC 33 basic Reflex save).
- Repulsion Gas (abjuration, arcane, incapacitation, mental); The dragon breathes a 80-foot line of repulsive gas. Each creature in the area must succeed at a DC 33 Will save or become fleeing from the dragon for 1 round (or 2 rounds on a critical failure).

**Draconic Frenzy** ◆◆ The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one tail Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum** When the dragon scores a critical hit with a Strike, it recharges its Breath Weapon.

**Water Mastery** (arcane, transmutation, water) For up to 60 minutes per day, the dragon, along with allied creatures and vessels within 50 feet, can move at double their normal Speed in water.
