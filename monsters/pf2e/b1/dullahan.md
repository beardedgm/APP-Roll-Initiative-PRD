**Head Hunter** Any slashing weapon a dullahan wields becomes keen, and any hatchet it wields becomes returning. If the dullahan kills a creature with a critical hit using a slashing weapon, the target is decapitated as though the dullahan had used Reap on the target. These effects remain only while the dullahan holds the weapon.

**Reap** ◆◆ The dullahan removes the head of a dead creature within reach. Each creature within the area of the dullahan's frightful presence must attempt a new save, even if they are temporarily immune.

**Summon Steed** ◆◆ (conjuration, occult) The dullahan summons a war horse with elite adjustments (page 6) and the fiend trait. This steed remains until it is slain, the dullahan Dismisses it, or the dullahan summons another steed.
