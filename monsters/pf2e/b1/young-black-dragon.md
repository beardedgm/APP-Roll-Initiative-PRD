# Young Black Dragon
*Creature 7*

ce, large, acid, amphibious, dragon

---

**Perception** +15; darkvision, scent 60 feet (imprecise)

**Languages** draconic

**Skills** Acrobatics +14, Arcana +12, Athletics +17, Deception +15, Intimidation +15, Stealth +16

**Recall Knowledge - Dragon** (Arcana): DC 23

**STR** +6, **DEX** +3, **CON** +4, **INT** +1, **WIS** +2, **CHA** +2

---

**AC** 25

**Fort** +17, **Ref** +12, **Will** +15

**HP** 125

**Immunities** acid, paralyzed, sleep

**Frightful Presence** (aura, emotion, fear, mental) 90 feet, DC 23

**Tail Lash** ◈ **Trigger** A creature within reach of the dragon's tail takes an action to Strike or attempt a skill check; The dragon Strikes with its tail at the triggering creature at a –2 penalty. If it hits, the creature takes a –2 circumstance penalty to the triggering roll.

Speed 40 feet, fly 100 feet, swim 40 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +19/+14/+9 (acid, reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 2d10+9 piercing plus 1d6 acid

**Melee** ◆ claw +19/+15/+11 (agile); **Damage** 2d6+9 slashing

**Melee** ◆ tail +17/+12/+7 (reach <15 feet>); **Damage** 2d8+7 bludgeoning

**Melee** ◆ horns +17/+12/+7 (reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 1d8+7 piercing

**Breath Weapon** ◆◆ (acid, arcane, evocation) The dragon breathes a spray of acid that deals 8d6 acid damage in a 60-foot line (DC 25 basic Reflex save). It can't use Breath Weapon again for 1d4 rounds.

**Draconic Frenzy** ◆◆ The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one horns Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum** The dragon recharges its Breath Weapon whenever it scores a critical hit with a Strike.
