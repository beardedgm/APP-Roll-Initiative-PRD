# Megalodon
*Creature 9*

uncommon, n, gargantuan, animal, aquatic

---

**Perception** +20; blood scent, scent 100 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Athletics +21, Stealth +19, Survival +16

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 28

**STR** +8, **DEX** +2, **CON** +5, **INT** -4, **WIS** +3, **CHA** -2

**Blood Scent** The shark can smell blood in the water from up to 1 mile away.

---

**AC** 27

**Fort** +21, **Ref** +16, **Will** +17

**HP** 180

---

Speed swim 80 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +22/+17/+12 (reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 2d12+10 piercing plus Improved Grab plus Improved Grab

**Melee** ◆ tail +22/+18/+14 (agile, reach <15 feet>); **Damage** 2d8+10 piercing plus Push 15 feet

**Breach** ◆◆ (attack, move) The megalodon Swims up to its swim Speed, then Leaps vertically out of the water up to 25 feet high, making a Strike against a creature at any point during the jump (this lets it attack a creature within 35 feet of the water's surface or 40 feet with its tail). After the Strike, the shark splashes back down into the water.

**Savage** ◆ **Requirements** The megalodon hit with a jaws Strike on its most recent action this turn.; The creature the megalodon hit takes 2d12 slashing damage.

**Swallow Whole** ◆ (attack) Huge, 2d8+5 bludgeoning, Rupture 20.
