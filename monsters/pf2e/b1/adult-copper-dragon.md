# Adult Copper Dragon
*Creature 12*

cg, large, dragon, earth

---

**Perception** +23; darkvision, scent 60 feet (imprecise)

**Languages** common, draconic, elven, gnomish

**Skills** Acrobatics +21, Athletics +24, Crafting +22, Deception +21, Performance +23, Society +22, Stealth +21

**Recall Knowledge - Dragon** (Arcana): DC 30

**STR** +6, **DEX** +3, **CON** +4, **INT** +4, **WIS** +3, **CHA** +5

---

**AC** 33

**Fort** +23, **Ref** +22, **Will** +22; +1 status to all saves vs. magic

**HP** 235

**Immunities** acid, paralyzed, sleep

**Frightful Presence** (aura, emotion, fear, mental) 90 feet, DC 29

**Twisting Tail** ◈ **Trigger** A creature within reach of the copper dragon's tail uses a move action or leaves a square during a move action it's using.; The dragon makes a tail Strike at the creature with a –2 penalty. If it hits, the dragon disrupts the triggering action.

---

Speed 40 feet, fly 140 feet, climb stone 40 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +26/+21/+16 (acid, magical, reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 3d8+12 piercing plus 2d8 acid

**Melee** ◆ claw +26/+22/+18 (agile, magical); **Damage** 3d8+12 slashing

**Melee** ◆ tail +24/+19/+14 (magical, reach <15 feet>); **Damage** 2d8+10 bludgeoning

**Innate arcane Spells** (DC 33)

  **1st** grease (at will)

  **4th** hideous laughter (at will), shape stone

**Breath Weapon** ◆◆ The copper dragon breathes in one of two ways. The dragon can't use Breath Weapon again for 1d4 rounds. - Acid (acid, arcane, evocation); The dragon breathes acid in a 80-foot line that deals 13d6 acid damage (DC 32 basic Fortitude save).
- Slowing Gas (arcane, transmutation); The dragon breathes a 80-foot line of slowing gas. Each creature in the area must succeed at a DC 32 Fortitude save or be slowed 1 for 1 round (or slowed 2 on a critical failure).

**Climb Stone** The dragon's climb speed functions only when climbing stone surfaces.

**Draconic Frenzy** ◆◆ The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one tail Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum** When the dragon scores a critical hit with a Strike, it recharges Breath Weapon.
