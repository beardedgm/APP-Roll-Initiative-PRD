# Lion
*Creature 3*

n, large, animal

---

**Perception** +9; low-light vision, scent 30 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Acrobatics +8, Athletics +11, Stealth +10

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 18

**STR** +4, **DEX** +3, **CON** +2, **INT** -4, **WIS** +2, **CHA** -2

---

**AC** 19

**Fort** +9, **Ref** +10, **Will** +7

**HP** 45

---

Speed 30 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +11/+6/+1; **Damage** 1d10+6 piercing plus Grab plus Grab

**Melee** ◆ claw +11/+7/+3 (agile); **Damage** 1d8+6 slashing

**Pack Attack** The lion deals 1d4 extra damage to any creature that's within reach of at least two of the lion's allies.

**Pounce** ◆ The lion Strides and makes a Strike at the end of that movement. If the lion began this action hidden, it remains hidden until after the ability's Strike.

**Sneak Attack** The lion deals 1d6 extra precision damage to flat-footed creatures.
