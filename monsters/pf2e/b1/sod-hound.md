# Sod Hound
*Creature 3*

n, small, earth, elemental

---

**Perception** +9; crystal sense 60 feet (imprecise), darkvision

**Skills** Athletics +11, Survival +9

**Recall Knowledge - Elemental** (Arcana, Nature): DC 18

**STR** +4, **DEX** -1, **CON** +3, **INT** -4, **WIS** +2, **CHA** -1

**Crystal Sense** A sod hound can sense crystals or gems within 60 feet as if using the scent ability.

---

**AC** 19

**Fort** +12, **Ref** +6, **Will** +7

**HP** 44

**Immunities** bleed, paralyzed, poison, sleep

---

Speed 30 feet, burrow 20 feet, earth glide

**Melee** ◆ jaws +11/+6/+1; **Damage** 1d10+6 piercing plus Knockdown plus Knockdown

**Earth Glide** The sod hound can Burrow through any earthen matter, including rock. When it does so, the sod hound moves at its full burrow Speed, leaving no tunnels or signs of its passing.
