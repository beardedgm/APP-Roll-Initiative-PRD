# Ball Python
*Creature 1*

n, medium, animal

---

**Perception** +6; low-light vision, scent 30 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Acrobatics +6, Athletics +6, Stealth +6, Survival +4

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 15

**STR** +3, **DEX** +3, **CON** +3, **INT** -4, **WIS** +1, **CHA** -2

---

**AC** 16

**Fort** +8, **Ref** +10, **Will** +4

**HP** 20

**Tighten Coils** ◈ **Trigger** A creature grabbed or restrained by the ball python attempts to Escape.; The DC of the Escape check is increased by 2.

---

Speed 20 feet, swim 20 feet, climb 20 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +8/+3/-2; **Damage** 1d8+3 piercing plus Grab plus Grab

**Constrict** ◆ 1d8 bludgeoning, DC 17

**Wrap in Coils** ◆ **Requirements** A Medium or smaller creature is grabbed or restrained in the ball python's jaws.; The ball python moves the creature into its coils, freeing its jaws to make attacks, then uses Constrict against the creature. The ball python's coils can hold one creature.
