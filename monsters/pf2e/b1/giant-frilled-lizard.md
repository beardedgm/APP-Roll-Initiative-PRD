# Giant Frilled Lizard
*Creature 5*

n, large, animal

---

**Perception** +11; low-light vision, scent 30 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Acrobatics +10, Athletics +14, Stealth +10

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 20

**STR** +5, **DEX** +1, **CON** +4, **INT** -4, **WIS** +2, **CHA** +0

---

**AC** 22

**Fort** +13, **Ref** +12, **Will** +9

**HP** 75

---

Speed 30 feet, climb 30 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +16/+11/+6 (reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 2d8+7 piercing

**Melee** ◆ tail +16/+12/+8 (agile, reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 2d6+7 bludgeoning

**Intimidating Display** ◆◆ (auditory, emotion, fear, mental, visual) The giant frilled lizard Strides. If it ends that Stride adjacent to at least one other creature, it suddenly displays its colorful neck frills and issues a frightening hiss. Each adjacent creature must succeed at a DC 21 Will save or become frightened 2 (or frightened 3 on a critical failure). The creature is then temporarily immune to Intimidating Display for 1 minute.
