# Great White Shark
*Creature 4*

n, huge, animal, aquatic

---

**Perception** +11; blood scent, scent 100 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Athletics +14, Stealth +12, Survival +9

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 19

**STR** +6, **DEX** +2, **CON** +4, **INT** -4, **WIS** +1, **CHA** -4

**Blood Scent** The shark can smell blood in the water from up to 1 mile away.

---

**AC** 21

**Fort** +12, **Ref** +10, **Will** +9

**HP** 60

---

Speed swim 40 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +14/+9/+4; **Damage** 1d12+8 piercing

**Breach** ◆◆ The shark Swims up to its swim Speed, then Leaps vertically out of the water up to 25 feet high, making a Strike against a creature at any point during the jump (this lets it attack a creature within 30 feet of the water's surface). After the Strike, the shark splashes back down into the water.

**Savage** ◆ **Requirements** The shark hit with a jaws Strike on its most recent action this turn.; The creature the shark hit takes 1d12 slashing damage.

**Strafing Chomp** ◆ The shark Swims up to half its swim Speed, makes a jaws Strike, and then Swims up to half its Speed further. The Strike deals half damage.
