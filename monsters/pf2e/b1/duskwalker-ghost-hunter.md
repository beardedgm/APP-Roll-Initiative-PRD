**Ghost Hunter** The duskwalker's weapons have the benefits of the ghost touch property rune on attacks against incorporeal undead.

**Hunt Prey** ◆ The duskwalker designates as its prey a single creature that it can see and hear or that it is Tracking. It gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks when it Seeks its prey and a +2 circumstance bonus to Survival checks when it Tracks its prey. It also ignores the penalty for making ranged attacks within its second range increment against its prey. Lastly, it gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Deception checks, Intimidation checks, and Stealth checks against its prey and to any check to Recall Knowledge about its prey, as well as a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against its prey's attacks. The duskwalker can have only one prey designated at a time.
