# Hyena
*Creature 1*

n, medium, animal

---

**Perception** +6; low-light vision, scent 30 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Acrobatics +6, Athletics +7, Stealth +6

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 15

**STR** +3, **DEX** +3, **CON** +2, **INT** -4, **WIS** +1, **CHA** -2

---

**AC** 16

**Fort** +7, **Ref** +8, **Will** +4

**HP** 20

---

Speed 40 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +8/+3/-2; **Damage** 1d8+3 piercing plus Knockdown plus Knockdown

**Drag** ◆ The hyena makes a jaws Strike against a prone enemy. If it hits, in addition to dealing damage, the hyena Strides up to 10 feet, dragging the enemy along.

**Pack Attack** The hyena deals an extra 1d4 damage to any creature that's within reach of at least two of the hyena's allies.
