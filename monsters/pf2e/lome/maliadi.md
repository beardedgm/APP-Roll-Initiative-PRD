**Collar of Fire** (abjuration, fire, primal) The maliadi has resistance 20 to physical ranged attacks, such as arrows, bolts, javelins, and thrown rocks, as such attacks are magically diverted through its collar of fire. Nonmagical weapons and ammunition affected by this ability are incinerated and destroyed.

**Counterspelling Wall** ◈ (evocation, fire, primal) **Trigger** A creature within 100 feet of the maliadi that the maliadi can see Casts a Spell that targets the maliadi; The maliadi casts one of its innate wall of fire spells, with a duration of 1 round. If the spell that triggered this reaction passes through the wall of fire, the wall attempts to counteract it, with a counteract modifier of +28.
