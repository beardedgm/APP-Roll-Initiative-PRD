# Ghoul
*Creature 1*

ce, medium, ghoul, undead

---

**Perception** +7; darkvision

**Skills** Acrobatics +7, Athletics +4, Stealth +7, Survival +5

**Recall Knowledge - Undead** (Religion): DC 15

**STR** +1, **DEX** +4, **CON** +1, **INT** +1, **WIS** +2, **CHA** +2

---

**AC** 16

**Fort** +4, **Ref** +9, **Will** +5

**HP** 20

**Immunities** poison, unconscious

---

Speed 30 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +9/+4/-1 (finesse); **Damage** 1d6 + 1 piercing plus paralysis plus paralysis

**Melee** ◆ claw +9/+5/+1 (agile, finesse); **Damage** 1d4 + 1 slashing plus paralysis plus paralysis

**Consume Flesh** ◆ (manipulate) The ghoul devours a chunk of a creature that died within the last hour and regains 1d6 Hit Points. The ghoul can regain Hit Points from a given body only once.

**Paralysis** (occult, necromancy) Any living creature (except an elf) that takes damage from the ghoul's Strike must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or gain the flat-footed condition for 1 round and lose its next turn. If the creature is level 3 or higher, this happens only on a critical failure.

**Swift Leap** ◆ The ghoul jumps up to half its Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions.
