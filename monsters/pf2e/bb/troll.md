# Troll
*Creature 5*

ce, large, giant, troll

---

**Perception** +11; darkvision

**Skills** Athletics +12, Intimidation +12

**STR** +5, **DEX** +2, **CON** +6, **INT** -2, **WIS** +0, **CHA** -2

---

**AC** 20

**Fort** +17, **Ref** +11, **Will** +7

**HP** 115; regeneration 20 (deactivated by acid or fire)

**Weaknesses** fire 10

**Regeneration** The troll regains 20 Hit Points at the start of their turn unless they've taken acid or fire damage since their last turn. The troll can't die unless their regeneration is deactivated when they're reduced to 0 HP.

---

Speed 30 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +14/+9/+4 (reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 2d10 + 5 piercing

**Melee** ◆ claw +14/+10/+6 (agile, reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 2d8 + 5 slashing

**Rend** ◆ If the troll succeeds at two claw Strikes against the same creature in the same turn, they can use this ability to automatically deal claw Strike damage to that creature.
