# Wight
*Creature 3*

le, medium, undead, wight

---

**Perception** +10; darkvision

**Skills** Athletics +11, Intimidation +9, Stealth +6

**Recall Knowledge - Undead** (Religion): DC 18

**STR** +4, **DEX** +1, **CON** +4, **INT** +0, **WIS** +3, **CHA** +2

---

**AC** 18

**Fort** +11, **Ref** +6, **Will** +10

**HP** 50

**Immunities** poison, unconscious

**Final Spite** ◈ When the wight is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it can use its reaction to make a Strike before being destroyed. It doesn't gain any Hit Points from drain life on this Strike.

---

Speed 25 feet

**Melee** ◆ claw +12/+7/+2; **Damage** 1d6 + 4 slashing plus drain life plus drain life

**Drain Life** (divine, necromancy) When the wight deals damage to a living creature with its claw Strike, the wight regains 2 Hit Points and the target must succeed at a DC 17 Fortitude save or take 2 damage that can't be healed. The wight can use drain life multiple times, but a single creature can't take more than 8 unhealable damage. Each full night of rest heals 2 of the unhealable damage, and a full day of bed rest increases the recovery to 4 of the unhealable damage.
