# Xulgath Boss
*Creature 3*

ce, medium, humanoid, xulgath

---

**Perception** +9; darkvision

**Skills** Athletics +11, Intimidation +6, Stealth +6

**Recall Knowledge - Humanoid** (Society): DC 18

**STR** +4, **DEX** +1, **CON** +2, **INT** -1, **WIS** +2, **CHA** +1

**Items** breastplate, greataxe, javelin (4)

---

**AC** 20

**Fort** +9, **Ref** +6, **Will** +9

**HP** 44

**Stench** A creature that comes within 30 feet of the xulgath and can smell the xulgath must attempt a DC 19 Fortitude save. On a failure, the creature takes a –1 status penalty to all checks and DCs until it uses an action to retch. A creature that succeeds at its save isn't affected by any xulgaths' stenches for 1 minute.

---

Speed 25 feet

**Melee** ◆ greataxe +11/+6/+1 (sweep); **Damage** 1d10 + 6 slashing

**Melee** ◆ jaws +11/+6/+1; **Damage** 1d6 + 6 piercing

**Melee** ◆ claw +11/+7/+3 (agile); **Damage** 1d4 + 6 slashing

**Ranged** ◆ javelin +8/+3/-2 (thrown <30 feet>); **Damage** 1d6 + 4 piercing

**Swipe** ◆◆ The xulgath boss makes two Strikes with their greataxe against two adjacent foes, both of whom are within their reach, with a +1 circumstance bonus. The second attack uses the same multiple attack penalty as the first, but attacks the xulgath boss makes after this take a –10 multiple attack penalty (or a –8 multiple attack penalty for its claw).
