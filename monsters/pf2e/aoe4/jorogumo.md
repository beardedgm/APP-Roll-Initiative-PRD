**HP** 270

**Darting Legs** ◈ **Trigger** The jorogumo is targeted with an attack; **Requirements** The jorogumo has their spider legs extended or has Changed Shape; The jorogumo raises a leg, gaining a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack.

**Innate occult Spells** (DC 34)

**Change Shape** ◆ (concentrate, occult, polymorph, transmutation) The jorogumo takes on the appearance of any Small or Medium spider. This doesn't change their Speed or Strikes.

**Jorogumo Venom** (poison)

**Spider Legs** ◆ (concentrate, occult, polymorph, transmutation) **Requirements** The jorogumo is in humanoid form; Eight large spider legs sprout from the jorogumo's back, granting them a 40-foot climb Speed and allowing them to use the Darting Legs reaction.

**Web Trap** A creature hit by the jorogumo's web attack is immobilized and stuck to the nearest surface, preventing the creature from moving. The DC to Escape or Force Open the web trap is 32.
