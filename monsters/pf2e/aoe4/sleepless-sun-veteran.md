**Melee** ◆ temple sword +16/+11/+6 (trip); **Damage** 2d8+8 slashing

**Ranged** ◆ composite shortbow +14/+9/+4 (deadly <1d10>, propulsive, range <60 feet>, reload <0>); **Damage** 1d6+4 piercing

**Power Attack** ◆◆ The veteran makes a melee Strike that counts as two attacks when calculating their multiple attack penalty. If this strike hits, it deals an extra die of weapon damage.

**Swipe** ◆◆ The veteran makes a melee Strike and compares the attack roll result to the AC of up to two foes, each of whom must be within their melee reach and adjacent to each other. Roll damage only once and apply it to each creature hit. A Swipe counts as two attacks for the veteran's multiple attack penalty.
