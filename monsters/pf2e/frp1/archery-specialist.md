**Pinning Shot** ◆ The archery specialist makes a longbow Strike. If the attack hits, it deals no damage and instead imparts the bow critical specialization effect. If the attack is a critical hit, the target must succeed at two separate Interact actions to pull the missile free.

**Point-Blank Shot** The archery specialist ignores the penalty to their attack rolls from the volley trait.

**Rain of Arrows** ◆◆ (flourish) The archery specialist fires several arrows at once, each aimed to land in a 10-foot burst within 60 feet. The archery specialist makes a longbow Strike and compares the attack roll result to.

**the** AC of all creatures in the burst. The specialist rolls damage only once and applies it to each creature hit.

**Rain of** Arrows counts as three attacks for the archery specialist's multiple attack penalty.
