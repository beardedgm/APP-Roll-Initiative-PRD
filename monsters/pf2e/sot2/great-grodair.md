# Great Grodair
*Creature 7*

unique, cn, large, amphibious, beast, fey, water

---

**Perception** +16; darkvision

**Languages** aquan, common, sylvan

**Skills** Athletics +17, Intimidation +15, Nature +15, Performance +17, Survival +14

**Recall Knowledge - Beast** (Arcana, Nature): DC 33

**STR** +5, **DEX** +1, **CON** +4, **INT** +1, **WIS** +2, **CHA** +4

---

**AC** 23

**Fort** +18, **Ref** +12, **Will** +14

**HP** 130

**Death Flood** (conjuration, primal, water) When the great grodair dies, his body explodes in a blast of pressurized water that deals 6d6 bludgeoning damage to creatures within a 15-foot emanation (DC 25 basic Reflex save). A creature that fails its save is pushed 5 feet (or 10 feet on a critical failure).

---

Speed 25 feet, swim 50 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +18/+13/+8; **Damage** 2d10+9 piercing

**Melee** ◆ tentacle +18/+13/+8 (reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 1d12+9 bludgeoning plus Knockdown plus Knockdown

**Ranged** ◆ water jet +16/+11/+6 (range increment <60 feet>); **Damage** 4d6 bludgeoning plus Push 10 feet

**Spontaneous primal Spells** (DC 25)

  **5th** control water

**Muddy Field** ◆ (primal, transmutation, water) The great grodair transforms all soil, sand, or similar sediment in a 10-foot emanation into mud for 1 round. This mud is terrain for creatures other than grodairs.

**Organ of Endless Water** ◆ (conjuration, manipulate, primal, water) The great grodair causes water to pour from a magical sac on his spine, either a stream of water at a rate of 1 gallon per round, or a fountain in a 5-foot-long stream at a rate of 5 gallons per round. He can stop the flow of water as a single action.
