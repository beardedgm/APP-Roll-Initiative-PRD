**Pack Attack** A gnoll deals 1d4 extra damage to any creature that's within reach of at least two of the gnoll's allies.

**Stealthy Abduction** When a Sludgespine killer successfully Grapples a creature, that creature makes no sound; other creatures can't notice the grapple by hearing alone. As long as it's grappled, the creature can't use actions with the auditory trait or cast spells with a verbal component.
