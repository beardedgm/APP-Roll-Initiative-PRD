**Silver Shower Shot** ◆ **Frequency** {"number":1,"unit":"round"}; Dresh throws 2d6 silver pieces into the air, potentially distracting her target. If her flintlock pistol is loaded, Dresh then makes a ranged flintlock pistol Strike against that target.

**Sneak Attack** Dresh deals an additional 1d6 precision damage to flat-footed creatures.
