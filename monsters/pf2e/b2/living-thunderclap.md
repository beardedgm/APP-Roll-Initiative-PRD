# Living Thunderclap
*Creature 4*

n, medium, air, elemental

---

**Perception** +9; darkvision

**Languages** auran

**Skills** Acrobatics +12, Stealth +12

**Recall Knowledge - Elemental** (Arcana, Nature): DC 19

**STR** +3, **DEX** +4, **CON** +2, **INT** -3, **WIS** +1, **CHA** +0

---

**AC** 22

**Fort** +10, **Ref** +12, **Will** +9

**HP** 50

---

Speed fly 50 feet, swiftness

**Melee** ◆ gust +14/+10/+6 (agile, finesse); **Damage** 2d6+6 bludgeoning plus Push 5 feet

**Ranged** ◆ lightning bolt +14/+9/+4 (electricity, range increment <50 feet>); **Damage** 2d12 electricity

**Swiftness** The living thunderclap doesn't trigger reactions when it moves.

**Thunderbolt** ◆◆ (electricity, evocation, primal, sonic) The living thunderclap emits a bolt of lightning that crashes with deafening thunder. The living thunderclap makes a lightning bolt Strike that deals 1d12 electricity damage. If it hits, the target and any creatures within a 15-foot emanation around the target take 2d6 sonic damage and must attempt a DC 18 basic Fortitude save. Any creature that fails its save is also deafened for 1d4 rounds.
