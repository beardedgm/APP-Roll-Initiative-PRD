n, medium, animal

**Perception** +17

**STR** +4, **DEX** +5, **CON** +2, **INT** -5, **WIS** +2, **CHA** -5

---

**Fort** +14, **Ref** +17, **Will** +12

**HP** 80

Speed 20 feet, fly 50 feet

**Melee** proboscis +17 (finesse); **Damage** 2d10+7 piercing plus Grab and septic malaria plus Grab, septic malaria

**Blood Drain** ◆ **Requirements** The giant mosquito has a creature grabbed; The giant mosquito uses its proboscis to drain blood from the grabbed creature. This deals 3d6 damage, and the giant mosquito gains temporary Hit Points equal to the damage dealt. A creature that has its blood drained by a giant mosquito is drained 1 until it receives healing of any kind or amount.

**Septic Malaria** (disease)
