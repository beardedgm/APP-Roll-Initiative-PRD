# Amoeba Swarm
*Creature 1*

n, large, amphibious, mindless, ooze, swarm

---

**Perception** +3; motion sense 60 feet, no vision

**Skills** Stealth +1

**Recall Knowledge - Ooze** (Occultism): DC 15

**STR** +0, **DEX** -2, **CON** +3, **INT** -5, **WIS** +0, **CHA** -5

**Motion Sense** An amoeba swarm can sense nearby creatures through vibration and air or water movement.

---

**AC** 9

**Fort** +8, **Ref** +1, **Will** +3

**HP** 35

**Immunities** acid, mental, precision, critical hits, unconscious, visual

---

Speed 5 feet, swim 10 feet, climb 5 feet

**Swarming Slither** ◆ The amoeba swarm slithers over each creature in its space, dealing 1d6 acid damage (DC 14 basic Reflex save). A creature that critically fails is sickened 1.

**Weak Acid** An amoeba's acid damages only organic material—not metal, stone, or other inorganic substances.
