**Mohrg Spawn** (occult, necromancy) A living creature slain by a mohrg that had a lower level than the mohrg rises as a mohrg spawn|B2 after 1d4 rounds, on its turn. This mohrg spawn|B2 is under the command of the mohrg that created it. If the creator of the mohrg spawn|B2 is destroyed, the mohrg spawn|B2 is destroyed as well, immediately collapsing into a pile of decayed flesh and bones.

**Paralysis** (occult, incapacitation, necromancy) A living creature hit by a mohrg's tongue Strike must succeed at a DC 26 Fortitude save or become paralyzed. The creature can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns, and the DC cumulatively decreases by 1 on each attempt.
