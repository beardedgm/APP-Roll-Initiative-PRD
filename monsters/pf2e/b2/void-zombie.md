# Void Zombie
*Creature 1*

rare, n, medium, undead

---

**Perception** +3; darkvision

**Skills** Athletics +6

**Recall Knowledge - Undead** (Religion): DC 20

**STR** +3, **DEX** -2, **CON** +2, **INT** -5, **WIS** +0, **CHA** -2

---

**AC** 13

**Fort** +7, **Ref** +3, **Will** +5

**HP** 26; negative healing

**Immunities** poison, death effects, disease, paralyzed

**Weaknesses** salt water 5, slashing 2

**Salt Water Vulnerability** Salt water acts as an extremely strong acid on the larval akata inside the void zombie. Full immersion in salt water deals 4d6 acid damage per round. On any round in which the void zombie takes damage due to its salt water weakness, the larval akata retreats to the depths of the void zombie's body, causing the void zombie to become slowed 1 until the end of its next turn.

---

Speed 25 feet

**Melee** ◆ fist +8/+3/-2; **Damage** 1d6+3 bludgeoning

**Melee** ◆ feeding tendril +6/+2/-2 (agile); **Damage** 1d4+3 piercing plus Feed on Blood plus Feed on Blood

**Feed on Blood** ◆ **Requirements** The void zombie's previous action was a successful feeding tendril Strike against the target creature; The void zombie uses its feeding tendril to drain blood from the creature struck. The void zombie regains 4 Hit Points, and the creature is drained 1 until it receives healing of any kind or amount.
