# Grippli Scout
*Creature 1*

n, small, grippli, humanoid

---

**Perception** +8; darkvision

**Languages** common, grippli

**Skills** Acrobatics +7, Athletics +4, Nature +6, Stealth +7, Survival +6

**Recall Knowledge - Humanoid** (Society): DC 15

**STR** +1, **DEX** +4, **CON** +2, **INT** +0, **WIS** +3, **CHA** -1

**Items** dart (5), leather armor, net, sickle

---

**AC** 18

**Fort** +7, **Ref** +9, **Will** +6

**HP** 20

---

Speed 25 feet, climb 20 feet, jungle stride

**Melee** ◆ sickle +9/+5/+1 (agile, finesse, trip); **Damage** 1d4+1 slashing

**Ranged** ◆ dart +9/+5/+1 (agile, thrown <20 feet>); **Damage** 1d4+1 piercing

**Hurl Net** ◆ **Requirements** The grippli is wielding a net in two hands; The grippli makes a ranged Strike (with a +9 modifier) against a Medium or smaller creature within 20 feet. On a hit, the target is flat-footed and takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds. On a critical hit, the creature is restrained instead. The DC to Escape the net is 16. A creature adjacent to the target can Interact with the net to remove it.

**Jungle Stride** Gripplis ignore terrain in forests and jungles.
