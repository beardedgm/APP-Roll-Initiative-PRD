**Deflecting Gale** ◈ (air, evocation, primal) **Trigger** The sylph sneak is the target of a physical ranged attack; **Requirements** The sylph sneak is aware of the attack; A swift gale whips up between the sylph sneak and the source of the ranged attack, giving the sneak a +3 status bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses, the wind deflected it. The wind can't deflect unusually large or heavy ranged projectiles (such as boulders or ballista bolts).

**Ranged** ◆ starknife +9/+5/+1 (agile, deadly <d6>, thrown <30 feet>, versatile <S>); **Damage** 1d4+1 plus 1d4+1

**Sneak Attack** The sylph sneak's Strikes deal 1d6 extra precision damage to flat-footed creatures.

**Surprise Attacker** On the first round of combat, creatures that haven't acted yet are flat-footed to the sylph sneak.

**Wind's Guidance** When the sylph sneak attacks with a thrown weapon, the range increment increases by 10 feet.
