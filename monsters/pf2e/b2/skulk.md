# Skulk
*Creature 1*

ce, medium, humanoid, skulk

---

**Perception** +5; low-light vision

**Languages** common

**Skills** Acrobatics +6, Deception +6, Society +4, Stealth +8, Thievery +6

**Recall Knowledge - Humanoid** (Society): DC 15

**STR** +0, **DEX** +3, **CON** +2, **INT** +1, **WIS** +2, **CHA** +1

**Items** dagger (2), shortsword

**Camouflaged Step** The skulk gains the benefit of the Cover Tracks action in forests and subterranean settings without moving at half Speed.

**Chameleon Skin** The skulk's skin shifts and changes to match the surroundings. As long as most of their body is not covered by clothing or armor, the skulk gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Stealth checks to Hide.

**AC** 16

**Fort** +7, **Ref** +8, **Will** +5

**HP** 21

**Melee** ◆ shortsword +8/+4/+0 (agile, finesse, versatile <S>); **Damage** 1d6+2 piercing

**Ranged** ◆ dagger +8/+4/+0 (agile, thrown <10 feet>, versatile <S>); **Damage** 1d4+2 piercing

**Sneak Attack** A skulk's Strikes deal an extra 1d6 precision damage to flat-footed creatures.
