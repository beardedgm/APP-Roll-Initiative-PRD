# Gray Ooze
*Creature 4*

n, medium, mindless, ooze

---

**Perception** +8; motion sense 60 feet, no vision

**Skills** Athletics +11, Stealth +10

**Recall Knowledge - Ooze** (Occultism): DC 19

**STR** +5, **DEX** +2, **CON** +4, **INT** -5, **WIS** +0, **CHA** -5

**Motion Sense** An amoeba swarm can sense nearby creatures through vibration and air or water movement.

**Puddled Ambush** ◈ **Trigger** A creature enters a space occupied by a gray ooze; **Requirements** Initiative has not yet been rolled; The gray ooze automatically notices the creature, then makes a pseudopod Strike against one creature adjacent to itself before rolling initiative.

---

**AC** 14

**Fort** +12, **Ref** +10, **Will** +8

**HP** 60

**Immunities** acid, mental, precision, critical hits, unconscious, visual

---

Speed 10 feet, climb 10 feet

**Melee** ◆ pseudopod +13/+9/+5 (agile); **Damage** 1d6+5 bludgeoning plus 1d6 acid and Grab plus Grab

**Constrict** ◆ 1d6 bludgeoning plus 1d6 acid, DC 21

**Gray Ooze Acid** A gray ooze's acid damages only metal and organic materials, not stone.
