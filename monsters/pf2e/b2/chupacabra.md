# Chupacabra
*Creature 3*

n, small, beast

---

**Perception** +9; darkvision

**Languages** aklo (can't speak any language)

**Skills** Acrobatics +9, Stealth +9

**Recall Knowledge - Beast** (Arcana, Nature): DC 18

**STR** +3, **DEX** +4, **CON** +2, **INT** -3, **WIS** +2, **CHA** -2

---

**AC** 20

**Fort** +9, **Ref** +11, **Will** +7

**HP** 45

---

Speed 25 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +11/+6/+1 (finesse); **Damage** 1d10+5 piercing plus Grab plus Grab

**Melee** ◆ claw +11/+7/+3 (agile, finesse); **Damage** 1d6+5 slashing

**Chupar** ◆ **Requirements** The chupacabra has a creature grabbed; The chupacabra sucks blood from the grabbed creature. The chupacabra gains the quickened condition for 1 minute and can use the extra action only for Strike and Stride actions. A chupacabra can't use Chupar again while it is quickened in this way. A creature that has its blood drained by a chupacabra is drained 1 until it receives healing (of any kind or amount).

**Pounce** ◆ The chupacabra Strides and makes a Strike at the end of that movement. If the chupacabra began this action hidden, it remains hidden until after this ability's Strike.
