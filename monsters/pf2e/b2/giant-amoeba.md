# Giant Amoeba
*Creature 1*

n, small, amphibious, mindless, ooze

---

**Perception** +4; motion sense 60 feet, no vision

**Skills** Athletics +6, Stealth +3

**Recall Knowledge - Ooze** (Occultism): DC 15

**STR** +3, **DEX** -2, **CON** +2, **INT** -5, **WIS** +0, **CHA** -5

**Motion Sense** An amoeba swarm can sense nearby creatures through vibration and air or water movement.

---

**AC** 8

**Fort** +7, **Ref** +3, **Will** +5

**HP** 45

**Immunities** acid, mental, precision, critical hits, unconscious, visual

---

Speed 10 feet, swim 10 feet, climb 10 feet

**Melee** ◆ pseudopod +8/+3/-2; **Damage** 1d6 acid plus Grab plus Grab

**Constrict** ◆ 1d4 bludgeoning plus 1d4 acid, DC 17

**Envelop** ◆◆◆ **Requirements** The giant amoeba begins its turn with a target its size or smaller grabbed; The giant amoeba maintains the Grab and extends pseudopods to surround the creature and pull it inside the amoeba's body. This thereafter has the same effect as if the amoeba had Engulfed the creature (DC 17, 1d6 acid, Escape DC 17, Rupture 3).

**Weak Acid** An amoeba's acid damages only organic material—not metal, stone, or other inorganic substances.
