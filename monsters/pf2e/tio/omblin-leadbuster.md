# Omblin Leadbuster
*Creature 2*

unique, ne, medium, dwarf, humanoid

---

**Perception** +12; darkvision

**Skills** Acrobatics +8, Society +5, Stealth +8, Survival +6, Thievery +8

**Recall Knowledge - Humanoid** (Society): DC 26

**STR** +2, **DEX** +4, **CON** +2, **INT** +1, **WIS** +2, **CHA** +0

**Items** hand crossbow (10 bolts), leather armor, rapier

---

**AC** 18

**Fort** +8, **Ref** +10, **Will** +8

**HP** 30

**Nimble Dodge** ◈ When a creature Omblin can see targets him with an attack, Omblin gains a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the attack.

---

Speed 20 feet

**Melee** ◆ rapier +11/+6/+1 (deadly <d8>, finesse); **Damage** 1d6+4 piercing

**Ranged** ◆ hand crossbow +11/+6/+1 (range increment <60 feet>, reload <1>); **Damage** 1d6+2 piercing

**Sneak Attack** Omblin deals an extra 1d6 to creatures that have the flat-footed condition.

**Unbalancing Blow** When Omblin critically hits a target and deals damage, the target is flat-footed against his attacks until the end of his next turn.

**Unburdened Iron** Omblin ignores the reduction to his Speed from armor and deducts 5 feet from any one penalty to his Speed (such as from a spell).
