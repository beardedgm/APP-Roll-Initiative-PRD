**Claw Frenzy** ◆◆ As xorn.

**Crystal Corruption** (arcane, curse, incapacitation, transmutation) Creatures afflicted by this curse slowly turn to solid crystal. This affliction's sickened, slowed, and paralyzed conditions can't be removed until the affliction itself is removed. Creatures with both the earth and elemental traits instead become carriers and suffer no ill effects aside from gaining the weakness to sonic. 

**Crystal Splinter** On a critical hit with a claw Strike, the crystalline sentinel inflicts 2d6 persistent bleed damage.
