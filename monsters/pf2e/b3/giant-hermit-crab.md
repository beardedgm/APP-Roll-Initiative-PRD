# Giant Hermit Crab
*Creature 5*

n, large, amphibious, animal

---

**Perception** +13; darkvision, {@ability tremorsense} 15 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Acrobatics +9, Athletics +15, Stealth +9

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 20

**STR** +6, **DEX** +2, **CON** +4, **INT** -4, **WIS** +2, **CHA** +0

---

**AC** 21

**Fort** +15, **Ref** +9, **Will** +9

**HP** 114

**Weaknesses** bludgeoning 5

**Protective Pinch** ◈ **Trigger** A creature adjacent to the hermit crab attempts to Strike the hermit crab while it is Retracted; The hermit crab makes a big claw Strike against the triggering creature.

---

Speed 20 feet, swim 15 feet, climb 10 feet

**Melee** ◆ big claw +15/+10/+5; **Damage** 2d8+8 piercing plus Grab plus Grab

**Melee** ◆ claw +15/+11/+7 (agile); **Damage** 2d4+8 piercing

**Constrict** ◆ 1d8+6 bludgeoning, DC 22

**Retract** ◆ The hermit crab curls fully into its shell to protect itself. This gives it a +4 circumstance bonus to AC, removes its weakness to bludgeoning damage, and grants it resistance 5 to piercing and slashing damage. The only actions the hermit crab can use while in this state are Protective Pinch and Interact to come out of its shell.
