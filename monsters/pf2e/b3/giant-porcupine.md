# Giant Porcupine
*Creature 2*

n, medium, animal

---

**Perception** +8; low-light vision, scent 30 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Athletics +8

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 16

**STR** +4, **DEX** +2, **CON** +4, **INT** -4, **WIS** +2, **CHA** +0

---

**AC** 18

**Fort** +10, **Ref** +8, **Will** +6

**HP** 32

**Passive Points** A creature that hits a porcupine with an unarmed Strike or a non-reach melee Strike takes 1d8 piercing damage (basic Reflex save) On a critical failure, the creature also takes 1d4 persistent piercing damage as the quills hook into its flesh.

---

Speed 25 feet

**Melee** ◆ quills +11/+6/+1; **Damage** 1d8+4 piercing plus embed quill plus embed quill

**Melee** ◆ tail +11/+6/+1; **Damage** 1d6+4 bludgeoning

**Embed Quill** A creature damaged by the porcupine's quills Strike must succeed at a DC 18 Reflex save or some of the quills remain embedded in its body, dealing 1d4 persistent piercing damage.

**Rearward Rush** ◆◆ The porcupine scuttles backward, quills extended. It Strides twice, then makes a quill Strike. The target is flat-footed against this Strike unless it has seen a Rearward Rush before.
