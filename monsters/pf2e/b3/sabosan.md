# Sabosan
*Creature 5*

ne, medium, humanoid

---

**Perception** +10; echolocation 20 feet, low-light vision, scent 30 feet (imprecise)

**Languages** abyssal, mwangi

**Skills** Acrobatics +16, Athletics +11, Stealth +16

**Recall Knowledge - Humanoid** (Society): DC 20

**STR** +4, **DEX** +5, **CON** +2, **INT** -1, **WIS** +1, **CHA** +0

**Items** spear

**Echolocation** A sabosan can use their hearing as a precise sense at the listed range.

---

**AC** 22

**Fort** +11, **Ref** +14, **Will** +10

**HP** 78

---

Speed 25 feet, fly 25 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +15/+10/+5 (finesse); **Damage** 2d10+4 piercing plus 1 persistent bleed

**Melee** ◆ claw +15/+11/+7 (agile, finesse); **Damage** 2d8+4 slashing plus Grab plus Grab

**Melee** ◆ spear +15/+10/+5; **Damage** 1d6+7 piercing

**Ranged** ◆ spear +16/+11/+6 (thrown <20 feet>); **Damage** 1d6+7 piercing

**Drain Blood** ◆ **Requirements** The sabosan has a creature grabbed; The sabosan drains blood from the creature. The creature must succeed at a DC 23 Fortitude save or become drained 1. The sabosan gains a number of temporary Hit Points equal to the number of Hit Points lost by the creature.

**Fell Shriek** ◆◆ (auditory) The sabosan emits a deafening cry in a 30-foot cone. Non-sabosan creatures in this area must each succeed at a DC 23 Fortitude save or be deafened for 1 minute.

**Powerful Charge** ◆◆ The sabosan Strides up to double their Speed and then makes a claw Strike. If the sabosan moved at least 20 feet, they deal an additional 1d6 damage on a hit.
