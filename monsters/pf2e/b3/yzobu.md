# Yzobu
*Creature 1*

n, large, animal

---

**Perception** +6; low-light vision, scent 30 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Athletics +7, Intimidation +5, Survival +4

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 15

**STR** +4, **DEX** +3, **CON** +4, **INT** -5, **WIS** +1, **CHA** +2

---

**AC** 16

**Fort** +9, **Ref** +6, **Will** +4

**HP** 25

**Pungent Aura** (aura, olfactory) 10 feet. Creatures other than yzobus or hobgoblins that enter or start their turn in the yzobu's aura must succeed at a DC 14 Fortitude save or become sickened 1, or sickened 2 on a critical failure. A creature that succeeds at its save is temporarily immune for 1 minute.
