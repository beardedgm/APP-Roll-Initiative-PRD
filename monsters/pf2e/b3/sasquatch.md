# Sasquatch
*Creature 2*

uncommon, n, medium, humanoid

---

**Perception** +8; low-light vision, scent 30 feet (imprecise)

**Languages** sasquatch

**Skills** Athletics +9, Intimidation +5, Stealth +9, Survival +6

**Recall Knowledge - Humanoid** (Society): DC 18

**STR** +5, **DEX** +1, **CON** +3, **INT** -1, **WIS** +2, **CHA** +1

---

**AC** 17

**Fort** +11, **Ref** +7, **Will** +6

**HP** 36

**Catch Rock** ◈

**Emerge From Undergrowth** ◇ **Trigger** The sasquatch is in forest terrain and rolls Stealth for Initiative; The sasquatch Strides up to half their Speed, after which they attempt to Demoralize a single creature within 15 feet.

---

Speed 30 feet

**Melee** ◆ fist +11/+6/+1; **Damage** 1d10+5 bludgeoning

**Ranged** ◆ rock +9/+4/-1 (brutal, range increment <30 feet>); **Damage** 1d6+5 bludgeoning

**Brutal Blows** On a critical hit with a fist Strike, the target is knocked prone by the blow.

**Forest Stride** The sasquatch ignores terrain in forests.

**Pungent** The sasquatch's odor is quite powerful and can be detected at twice the normal distance by scent.

**Threatening Visage** The sasquatch doesn't take a penalty to Demoralize a creature that doesn't understand their language.
