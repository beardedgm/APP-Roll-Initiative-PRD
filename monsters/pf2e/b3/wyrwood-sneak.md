# Wyrwood Sneak
*Creature 1*

rare, n, small, construct, wyrwood

---

**Perception** +6; darkvision

**Languages** common

**Skills** Acrobatics +7, Arcana +5, Deception +7, Society +5, Stealth +7

**Recall Knowledge - Construct** (Arcana, Crafting): DC 20

**STR** +0, **DEX** +4, **CON** +0, **INT** +2, **WIS** +1, **CHA** +2

**Items** buckler (Hardness 3, HP 6, BT 3), shortsword

---

**AC** 17

**Fort** +3, **Ref** +9, **Will** +8

**HP** 16

**Immunities** bleed

**Living Machine** Though their body is an organic construct, a wyrwood is a living creature. They're not immediately destroyed when reduced to 0 HP, but rather fall unconscious and eventually die. They don't need to eat or drink. They can be targeted by effects that target living creatures or that target constructs.

**No Breath** A wyrwood doesn't breathe and is immune to effects that require breathing (such as an inhaled poison).

---

Speed 20 feet

**Melee** ◆ shortsword +9/+5/+1 (agile, finesse, versatile <S>); **Damage** 1d6 piercing

**Sneak Attack** The wyrwood deals an additional 1d6 precision damage to flat-footed creatures.
