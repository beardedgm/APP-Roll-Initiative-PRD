*Creature 4*

n, large, animal, swarm

---

**Skills** Acrobatics +13, Stealth +11

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 19

**STR** +1, **DEX** +5, **CON** +3, **INT** -4, **WIS** +2, **CHA** -3

---

**AC** 18

**Fort** +11, **Ref** +13, **Will** +10

**HP** 50

**Venomous Fangs** ◆ Each enemy in the swarm's space takes 2d8 piercing damage (DC 21 basic Reflex save) plus viper swarm venom.
