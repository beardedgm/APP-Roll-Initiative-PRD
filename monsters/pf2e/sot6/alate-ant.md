**Frenzy** ◆ **Frequency** {"number":1,"unit":"day"}; The alate ant enters a state of jittery agitation. It becomes quickened for 3 rounds and can use the extra action only to Fly or Strike.

**Pack Attack** The alate ant deals an additional 3d6 precision damage to any creature within reach of at least two of the ant's allies.

**Red Ant Venom** (poison) As red guard ant|SoT6, but DC 37. While a creature is enfeebled from this poison, it is confused (this confusion is an emotion, enchantment, and mental effect).
