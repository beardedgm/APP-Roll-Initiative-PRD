**Envenomed Weapons** Weapons the wereant poisoner holds deal an additional 2d6 persistent poison damage as poison seeps from the wereant's hands onto the weapon. Weapons cease to deal this persistent poison damage once the wereant lets them go. The kukri Strikes above include this persistent poison damage.

**Poisonous Flurry** ◆◆ The wereant poisoner makes two kukri Strikes and a mandibles Strike in any order.

**Vitriolic Strikes** If the wereant critically hits a creature with a melee Strike, that creature can't recover from persistent poison damage for 1 round.
