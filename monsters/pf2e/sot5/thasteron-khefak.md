# Thasteron Khefak
*Creature 3*

uncommon, n, medium, beast

---

**Perception** +9; darkvision, tremorsense 60 feet (precise)

**Skills** Acrobatics +9, Athletics +10, Stealth +9

**Recall Knowledge - Beast** (Arcana, Nature): DC 20

**STR** +3, **DEX** +2, **CON** +3, **INT** -5, **WIS** +2, **CHA** -3

**Consume Objects** As khefak scuttler.

**Radiation Aura** (aura, radiation) 10 feet. Thasteron khefaks convert radioactive minerals they eat into thasteron, a combustive fuel (page 77). The more radioactive elements of the material concentrates in their chitin and taints the air around them. A creature who begins their turn within this aura must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude saving throw or become sickened 1 (sickened 2 on a critical failure).
