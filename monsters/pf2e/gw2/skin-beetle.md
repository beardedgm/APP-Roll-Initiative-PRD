# Skin Beetle
*Creature 3*

uncommon, n, tiny, animal

---

**Perception** +9; low-light vision

**Skills** Acrobatics +10, Medicine +9, Stealth +10

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 20

**STR** +1, **DEX** +4, **CON** +2, **INT** -5, **WIS** +2, **CHA** -2

**Surgical Mandibles** A skin beetle can use its mandibles as healer's tools.

---

**AC** 18

**Fort** +9, **Ref** +13, **Will** +7

**HP** 55

---

Speed 20 feet, fly 15 feet, burrow 15 feet

**Melee** ◆ mandibles +13/+9/+5 (agile, finesse); **Damage** 1d8+4 piercing

**Ranged** ◆ spit +13/+8/+3 (range <30 feet>); **Damage** 1d6+4 acid plus anesthetic saliva

**Anesthetic Saliva** (poison) A creature exposed to a skin beetle's saliva must succeed on a DC 19 Fortitude save or become clumsy 1 for 1 round.

**Harvest Flesh** ◆◆ The skin beetle makes a mandibles Strike against an animal or humanoid. If the Strike deals damage, the beetle tears off a large portion of flesh, dealing an additional 1d6 persistent bleed damage.
