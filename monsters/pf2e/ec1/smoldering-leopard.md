# Smoldering Leopard
*Creature 3*

uncommon, ne, medium, animal, fiend

---

**Perception** +11; darkvision, scent 30 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Acrobatics +11, Athletics +10, Stealth +11

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature, Religion): DC 20

**STR** +3, **DEX** +4, **CON** +2, **INT** -4, **WIS** +1, **CHA** -2

---

**AC** 19

**Fort** +9, **Ref** +11, **Will** +6

**HP** 45

**Resistances** electricity 5, poison 5

**Weaknesses** good 3

**Aura of Smoke** 10 feet. Every creature within the aura is concealed; smoldering leopards can see through this concealment.

---

Speed 30 feet, climb 20 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +12/+7/+2 (finesse); **Damage** 1d10+3 piercing plus 1d4 fire and Grab plus Grab

**Melee** ◆ claw +12/+8/+4 (agile, finesse); **Damage** 1d6+3 slashing plus 1d4 fire

**Pounce** ◆ The smoldering leopard Strides and makes a Strike at the end of that movement. If the leopard began this action hidden, it remains hidden until after making this ability's Strike.

**Scorching Maul** ◆ The smoldering leopard makes two claw Strikes against a creature it has grabbed. A creature hit by both claw Strikes also takes 1d4 persistent fire damage. Both attacks count toward its multiple attack penalty, but the penalty increases only after both attacks are made.

**Sneak Attack** (precision) The smoldering leopard deals an extra 1d4 precision damage to flat-footed creatures.
