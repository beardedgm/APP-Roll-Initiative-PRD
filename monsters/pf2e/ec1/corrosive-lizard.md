# Corrosive Lizard
*Creature 2*

uncommon, ne, medium, animal, fiend

---

**Perception** +7; darkvision, scent 30 feet (imprecise)

**Skills** Acrobatics +6, Athletics +8, Stealth +6

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature, Religion): DC 18

**STR** +2, **DEX** +2, **CON** +4, **INT** -4, **WIS** +1, **CHA** -2

---

**AC** 18

**Fort** +10, **Ref** +8, **Will** +5

**HP** 30

**Immunities** acid

**Weaknesses** good 3

**Gnashing Bite** ◈ **Trigger** A creature grabbed by the corrosive lizard's jaws fails a check to Escape; The lizard's jaws deal 1d6 piercing damage and 1d6 acid damage to the triggering creature.

---

Speed 30 feet, swim 30 feet

**Melee** ◆ jaws +11/+6/+1; **Damage** 1d10+2 piercing plus 1d6 persistent acid damage and Grab plus Grab

**Gout of Acid** ◆◆ (acid, primal) The corrosive lizard vomits a gout of acid in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in this area takes 3d6 acid damage (DC 20 basic Reflex save). The lizard can't use Gout of Acid again for 1d4 rounds.

**Lurching Charge** ◆◆ The corrosive lizard Strides up to double its Speed and then makes a jaws Strike. If the lizard moved at least 20 feet away from its starting position, it gains a +2 circumstance bonus to this attack roll.
