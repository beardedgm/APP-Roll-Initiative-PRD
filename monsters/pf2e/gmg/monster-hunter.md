**Rage** ◆ (concentrate, emotion, mental) **Requirements** The monster hunter isn't fatigued or raging; The monster hunter gains 9 temporary Hit Points that last until the rage ends. While raging, they deal 8 additional damage with melee attacks, gain a +10-foot status bonus to their Speed, and take a –1 penalty to AC. The monster hunter can't use concentrate actions except Seek and rage actions. The rage lasts for 1 minute, until there are no enemies the monster hunter can perceive, or until the monster hunter falls unconscious. Once the rage ends, the monster hunter can't Rage again for 1 minute.

**Sudden Charge** ◆◆ The monster hunter Strides twice and makes a melee Strike.
