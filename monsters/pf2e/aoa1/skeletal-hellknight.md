The creature struck feels as if nails are being driven into their body, and must succeed at a DC 17 Will save or be stunned|CRB|stunned 1 from the pain. On a critical failure, the pain manifests as actual puncture wounds, dealing an additional 2d6 points of piercing damage to the victim.
