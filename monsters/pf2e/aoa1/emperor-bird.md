Speed 15 feet, fly 20 feet

**Melee** ◆ beak +11/+6/+1; **Damage** 1d10+2 piercing

**Melee** ◆ tail +11/+7/+3 (agile, reach <10 feet>); **Damage** 1d8+3 slashing

**Dazzling Display** ◆◆ (enchantment, mental, primal, visual) The emperor bird flaps its mesmerizing wings. Each creature within 15 feet that can see the emperor bird must attempt a DC 19 Will save. **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Success** The creature is dazzled for 1 round.
**Failure** The creature is dazzled for 1d4 rounds.
**Critical Failure** The creature is confused for 1d4 rounds.

**Tail Lash** ◆◆ The emperor bird makes tail Strikes against two different creatures within reach. The emperor bird's multiple attack penalty does not increase until it has finished both attacks.
