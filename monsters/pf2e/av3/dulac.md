---

**AC** 28

**Fort** +15, **Ref** +18, **Will** +20

**HP** 155; death shadows

**Death Shadows** (cold, darkness) When Dulac dies, her body is destroyed in a burst of chilling shadows. All creatures in a 20-foot burst take 8d8 cold damage (DC 28 basic Reflex save). Dulac's gear and treasure are unaffected by the shadows and are left in a pile where she died.

**Opportune Step** ◈ **Trigger** A dazzled or blinded creature, or a creature with light blindness, ends its move adjacent to Dulac; Dulac Steps.

**Warding Shove** ◈ **Trigger** An ally within Dulac's reach is targeted with a ranged or melee attack by an attacker she can see; The ally gains a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack, and the ally can Step after the attack.
