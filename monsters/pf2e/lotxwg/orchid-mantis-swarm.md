# Orchid Mantis Swarm
*Creature 6*

large, animal, swarm

---

**Perception** +12; {@ability low-light vision}

**Skills** Stealth +16

**Recall Knowledge - Animal** (Nature): DC 22

**STR** -2, **DEX** +4, **CON** +1, **INT** -5, **WIS** +2, **CHA** -4

**Floral Camouflage** When Hiding, the orchid mantis swarm is concealed by any amount of leaves or petals, even if they aren't thick enough to make other creatures concealed.

---

**AC** 24

**Fort** +13, **Ref** +16, **Will** +12

**HP** 100

**Immunities** precision, {@ability swarm mind}

**Resistances** bludgeoning 2, piercing 5, slashing 5

**Weaknesses** area damage 5, splash damage 5

---

Speed 30 feet, fly 20 feet, climb 20 feet

**Biting Blossoms** ◆ Each enemy in the swarm's space takes 3d6 piercing damage (DC 24 basic Reflex save). Creatures that fail this save also take 1d4 persistent bleed damage.

**Drifting Petals** ◆ (mental, visual) The swarm Flies. Each creature whose space it moves through must attempt a DC 24 Will save or be transfixed by their blossom-like bodies. **Critical Success** No effect.
**Success**  The creature is dazzled for 1 round.
**Failure** The creature is dazzled and stupefied 1 for 1 round.
**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded and stupefied 2 for 1 round.
