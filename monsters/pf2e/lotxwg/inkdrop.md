# Inkdrop
*Creature -1*

tiny, construct, mindless

---

**Perception** +6; {@ability darkvision}

**Skills** Athletics +2, Stealth +3

**Recall Knowledge - Construct** (Arcana, Crafting): DC 13

**STR** +0, **DEX** -3, **CON** +2, **INT** -5, **WIS** +0, **CHA** -5

**Perfect Memory** An inkdrop remembers the exact pattern of any ink it moves over physically.

---

**AC** 7

**Fort** +2, **Ref** +6, **Will** +5

**HP** 15

**Immunities** critical hits, mental, precision, spirit, unconscious, vitality, void

**Weaknesses** cold 5, fire 5

**Blotting** While a creature has the inkdrop grabbed or pinned, it can destroy the inkdrop by smudging the inkdrop out with an Interact action.

---

Speed 10 feet, climb 10 feet

**Melee** ◆ pseudopod +6/+1/-4; **Damage** 1d4 bludgeoning plus blinding ink plus blinding ink

**Blinding Ink** A creature critically struck by the inkdrop's pseudopod is blinded by the ink until it clears its eyes using an Interact action.

**Ink Blot** ◆ The inkdrop leaves a pattern it remembers over a square foot of surface. If there's existing non-magical ink on the surface, the inkdrop erases the non-magical ink.
