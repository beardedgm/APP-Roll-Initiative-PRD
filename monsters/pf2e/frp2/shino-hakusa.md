**Ranged** ◆ shuriken +32/+28/+24 (agile, reload <0>, thrown <20 feet>); **Damage** 3d4+14 piercing

**Disorienting Tumble** Hakusa constantly leaps around and over her foes in combat. When she successfully Tumbles Through a creature's space, that creature becomes flat-footed until the end of Hakusa's turn.

**Powerful Fists** Hakusa's fists are treated as cold iron and silver and don't take penalties when making lethal attacks.

**Pressure Point Attack** ◆◆ Hakusa makes precise attacks that can debilitate a foe's movements. Hakusa makes a fist Strike. On a hit, the target takes damage as normal and must attempt a DC 38 Fortitude save. Regardless of the result of the save, the target is then temporarily immune to Pressure Point Attacks for 1 minute. **Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target's muscles become temporarily weaker. For 1 round, the target is enfeebled|CRB|enfeebled 2 and must succeed at a DC 5 flat check when attempting to use actions with the manipulate trait or the action is lost.
**Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 minute.
**Critical Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 minute, the flat check is DC 11, and the target also takes –10-foot status penalty to their Speed for the duration.

**Sneak Attack Hakusa's** Strikes deal an additional 4d6 precision damage to flat-footed creatures.
