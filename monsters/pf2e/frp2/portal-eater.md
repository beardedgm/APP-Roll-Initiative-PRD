# Portal Eater
*Creature 18*

rare, ce, gargantuan, astral

---

**Perception** +30; greater darkvision

**Languages** aklo; telepathy 100 feet

**Skills** Athletics +36, Intimidation +35

**Recall Knowledge - Astral** (Occultism): DC 43

**STR** +10, **DEX** +5, **CON** +8, **INT** +0, **WIS** +6, **CHA** +9

---

**AC** 37

**Fort** +34, **Ref** +29, **Will** +32

**HP** 420

**Resistances** all damage 10

**Weaknesses** force 20

**Enormous** A portal eater is a massive creature and takes up a space of 10 squares by 10 squares (50 feet by 50 feet).

**Eat Portal** ◈ (abjuration, occult) **Trigger** A creature teleports or is summoned within 50 feet of the portal eater; **Requirements** The portal eater's beak is not grabbing a creature; The portal eater attempts to eat the portal. It makes a counteract check against the effect (counteract modifier +33, counteract level 9th). If the effect is counteracted, the creature arrives in the portal eater's beak and the portal eater Grabs it.

**Reflect Spell** ◈ (abjuration, occult) **Trigger** A creature Casts a Spell that targets the portal eater; **Requirements** The portal eater's beak is not grabbing a creature; The portal eater attempts to counteract the spell (counteract modifier +32, counteract level 9th). If the spell is counteracted, it targets the caster instead.

**Melee** ◆ beak +34/+29/+24 (reach <25 feet>); **Damage** 3d12+20 piercing and Improved Grab plus Improved Grab

**Melee** ◆ claw +34/+30/+26 (agile, reach <30 feet>); **Damage** 3d8+20 slashing plus Improved Push 20 feet

**Melee** ◆ cloak +34/+29/+24 (reach <40 feet>); **Damage** 3d8+20 bludgeoning plus Pull 20 feet

**Swallow Whole** ◆ (attack) Gargantuan, 17d6+8 force, Rupture 33.

**Unfurl Realities** ◆◆ (evocation, light, occult) The portal eater unfurls its crystalline cloak of stolen realities, releasing a wave of cosmic energy and casting prismatic spray (DC 39) in a 60-foot emanation. The portal eater can't Unfurl Realities again for 1d4 rounds.
