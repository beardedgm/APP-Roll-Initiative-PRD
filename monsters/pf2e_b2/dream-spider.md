# Dream Spider
*Creature 0*

n, small, animal

---

**Perception** +6
**STR** +0, **DEX** +3, **CON** +1, **INT** -5, **WIS** +0, **CHA** -4

**Web Sense** The dream spider has imprecise tremorsense to detect the vibrations of creatures touching its web.

---

**AC** 16
**Fort** +5, **Ref** +7, **Will** +4
**HP** 15

---

Speed 25 feet, climb 25 feet

**Melee** bite +7 (finesse); **Damage** 1d6 plus dream spider venom plus 1d6, dream spider venom
**Melee** web +7 (range increment <10 feet>); plus web trap plus dream spider venom

**Dream Spider Venom** (poison)
**Web Trap** A creature hit by the dream spider's web attack is immobilized and stuck to the nearest surface until it Escapes (DC 16).
