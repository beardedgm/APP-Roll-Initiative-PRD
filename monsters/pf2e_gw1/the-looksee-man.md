**HP** 70

**Shadow Blending** When the Looksee Man is concealed as a result of dim light, the flat check to target him has a DC of 7, not 5.

**Stalk Goggles Specialist** As long as he wears his greater stalk goggles, the Looksee Man can't be flanked by creatures of 4th level or lower.

**Shadow Stride** ◆ (illusion, occult, shadow) **Requirements** The Looksee Man is in dim light; The Looksee Man Strides. He has a +10-foot status bonus to his Speed during this Stride. The DC from shadow blending increases to 11 during this Stride, and the Looksee Man remains concealed by dim light until the end of the movement, even if he leaves dim light during the Stride.
