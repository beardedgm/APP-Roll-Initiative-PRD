# Wolliped
*Creature 3*

n, large, animal

---

**Perception** +9
**STR** +3, **DEX** +4, **CON** +4, **INT** -4, **WIS** +1, **CHA** +2

---

**AC** 19
**Fort** +10, **Ref** +12, **Will** +5
**HP** 55

---

Speed 35 feet

**Melee** tusk +10; **Damage** 1d10+5 piercing
**Melee** hoof +10; **Damage** 1d8+5 bludgeoning
**Melee** spit +12 (range increment <30 feet>); **Damage** 1d8+2 bludgeoning

**Ice Stride** A wolliped isn't impeded by terrain caused by snow or ice, nor does it need to attempt Acrobatics checks to keep from falling on slippery ice.
**Regurgitated Wrath** ◆ Frequency once per hour; The wolliped regurgitates the contents of its stomach and spits them at the target, dealing 2d6 bludgeoning damage and 2d6 acid damage. The target attempts a DC 20 basic Reflex save, and on a failure also becomes sickened 1 (sickened 2 on a critical failure).
**Trample** ◆◆◆ Medium or smaller, hoof, DC 20
